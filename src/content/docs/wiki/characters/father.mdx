**Robert Lord** is the name used for Daniel's father in at least one public
r/HIMRFAM post title. The primary HIMR archive itself uses only **“Dad”** or **“my
dad”** in the located titles, so this page treats Robert Lord as a searchable
community identifier rather than an authenticated legal or preferred name.

### First-party date lead

In the first paragraph of a privately retained Patreon body for a post published on
**9 February 2019 at 20:47 UTC**, Daniel wrote that it was the third day since his
father had died. Depending on whether the day of death was counted inclusively and
which local timezone applied, that contemporaneous self-report supports approximately
**6–7 February 2019**. It remains an attributed date window, not a date verified
through a death certificate or other civil record.

## Retained compilation cross-check

Four public 2020 videos from the fan-run HIMR Database channel were acquired by exact
YouTube ID, normalized to mono audio, passed through a pinned `small.en` model, and
sampled visually. They preserve edited excerpts from several Daniel uploads; they are
useful clip finders and a reproducible cross-check, but they are not independent
witnesses and their edit order cannot prove a chronology.

The machine pass over _Distant Relations II_ renders Daniel giving a compact family
history at approximately **00:00–02:23**. He says his father sometimes travelled for
work when Daniel and his sister were children, that the family later moved into his
paternal grandfather's house, and that his father spent a period after being laid off
renovating it. Daniel says his father later returned to work but continued the house
work on weekends. These are Daniel's autobiographical claims, not verified employment,
property, or family records.

_The Unsung Hero I_ renders Daniel saying that his father checked on and listened to
him during an early breakup. At approximately **01:00–03:04**, Daniel says he then
worked for about five months at his father's company, disliked the role, quit, and had
saved roughly **£6,000**, which helped make a Japan trip possible. Later excerpts
render him describing his father as practical, physically active, interested in
gardening and building rather than the internet, and generally supportive of Daniel's
marriage and early, comparatively tame YouTube work. The compilation jumps among
different source moments, so these claims must not be read as one continuous account.
