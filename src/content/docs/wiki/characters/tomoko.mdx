**Tomoko** is the community navigation label for the woman in Daniel's October 2017
online-girlfriend sequence. Unlike many lore names, this one has a narrow bridge to a
public HIMR raw file: independent `base.en` and `small.en` passes both render Daniel
using **Tomoko** immediately before saying he is going to meet her for the first time.

Nobody directly heard that audio during this review. The name is therefore a strong,
benign machine-audio lead—not verified speech, a legal name, or permission to build a
private biography. Direct listening remains open in the maintainers' **Tomoko
audio-review queue**.

## Separately preserved response candidate

Internet Archive separately preserves a 450.79-second video titled [_Finally I Met
Daniel 💔_](https://archive.org/details/finally-i-met-daniel).
Its item metadata displays **Tomo L** as creator and `2017` as its date. Those are
archive metadata fields, not identity authentication.

Sampled raw frames visibly show a two-person selfie including Daniel around 00:30,
Daniel alone around 01:37, and an adult woman looking directly into the camera in a
composed sequence from about 01:43. This establishes a separately preserved,
first-person-looking response candidate showing Daniel and an adult woman. The archive's
**Tomo L** creator field is compatible with the claim that the woman published the
video herself, but metadata does not authenticate the original uploader or prove
that the woman is Tomoko. No facial comparison is used to corroborate Daniel's
allegations.

## Public Tomo L channel lead

The supplied [@tomol6417 YouTube address](https://www.youtube.com/@tomol6417) was
checked on 27 August 2026. It resolved to channel ID
`UCmLFDbW1DPXkdIRpVfC8Asw`, branded **Tomo L**, with 42 videos enumerable in a
credential-free flat-list capture. The shared display name makes the channel relevant
to the source trail, but it does not authenticate the channel holder as Tomoko, prove
that she is the woman in the response candidate, or establish common upload control
with the Internet Archive item.

A similarly worded live-channel upload must not be conflated with the response
candidate. [_2018…Finally, I met him_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zfsxzCeQBnY)
is a public 836-second 2018 upload whose description names another creator. The
separately archived _Finally I Met Daniel_ candidate is 450.79 seconds and carries a
year-only `2017` archive label. No exact video match between the channel and that
archived candidate has been established.

## Current boundary

- Tomoko is a strong machine-audio source label, not an authenticated identity.
- The first-meeting video exists as one raw media identity under two catalogue titles.
- The separately preserved response candidate is not authenticated as Tomoko or as
  a response from the unidentified alleged subject; its audio account remains
  unpublished pending direct listening and sensitivity review.
- The live **Tomo L** channel is a relevant supplied lead, but neither its holder nor
  its relationship to the archived response candidate is authenticated.
- The community timeline's age, marriage, sexual-history, and “catfish” conclusions
  are not promoted beyond Daniel's explicitly labelled allegation title.
