### What the machine passes can—and cannot—add

Separate offline `base.en` and `small.en` passes materially agree that the opening
**00:00–00:37** is framed as a Q&A for the guest and contains a candidate statement
that it is her first livestream. They also agree that the guest begins answering
viewer prompts during **00:56–01:10**. Nobody directly listened to the raw audio for
this review, so this is a benign navigation lead, not a quotation or verified
biographical fact.

The opening contains private-family, financial, and health-related machine renderings
immediately afterward. Those are unnecessary to identify the appearance and are not
published. A later interval produced implausibly repetitive ASR output; it was
treated as a model failure and discarded without inferring its cause.

## Subreddit research boundary

Subreddit searches produced additional stories about Sabrina. One public repost was
reviewed as a possible route to a 2023 stream, but its audio could not be tied to an
exact range in a mapped raw archive video during a bounded fingerprint review.
Visual similarity to a room or clothing was not treated as enough. The repost's
account and commenters' speculation are therefore omitted from this article.

This is also why the page does not turn community descriptions of Sabrina's work,
relationships, opinions, or family circumstances into biography. A future addition
needs an exact archived raw file and range, narrow attribution, and the same privacy
review applied here.
