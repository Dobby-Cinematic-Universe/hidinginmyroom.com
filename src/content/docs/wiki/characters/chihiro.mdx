Chihiro is the public on-channel name used by an archived 2019 cooking title for its
on-camera participant. Relationship-themed titles surround that named appearance,
but the wiki does not convert unchecked transcript narration into a
verified marriage or divorce history. This page deliberately does not publish a
surname, employer, private contact details, or identifying information outside her
public video role.

### A direct account-to-archive-item bridge

There is a stronger link than the profile names alone. The currently playable
Chihiro K upload *Day in my life of single woman in japan / 日本人シングル女性の１日*
uses video ID `keaCN2NeIRw`. The HIMR source manifest maps that same ID, title, and
approximately 496-second duration to a retained raw file in Internet Archive item
`699992`. At 01:28, the archived copy visibly displays the title card “COOKING WITH
CHIHIRO / AVOCADO TOAST.”

This verifies that **one public Chihiro K channel upload is also preserved inside the
HIMR archive collection**. It still does not, by itself, prove that the channel owner
is the same on-camera participant in Daniel's separately archived 2019 *Cooking with
Chihiro* video. Archive inclusion is an item-level bridge, not a legal-identity or
same-person finding.

## What is intentionally omitted

The archive index contains later titles with conflicting, intimate, or accusatory
wording about Chihiro. They are not repeated here. A source title cannot by itself
establish another person's motives, private conduct, health, legal history, or current
life. The page also avoids private family details and does not use community
speculation to fill gaps.

## Page verification status

This profile is **source matched**. The archive identities and public-name title are
reconciled, and the reproduced frame plus limited cooking footage were visually
checked. The external YouTube and Instagram profiles are linked to each other, and
one public channel upload is reconciled to its copy in HIMR Archive 2. Their proposed
person-level connection to the 2019 HIMR participant remains unresolved. Spoken
identity, relationship history, and the divorce-labelled account have not been
independently audio-checked or corroborated by civil records.
