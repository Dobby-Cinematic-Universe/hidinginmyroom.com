Chihiro is the public on-channel name used by an archived 2019 cooking title for the
woman it presents as Chihiro. Relationship-themed titles surround that named appearance,
and this page now records two narrow, conspicuously unverified accounts attributed to
Daniel where separate ASR passes materially agree. They are not a verified marriage
or divorce history and do not identify the unnamed wife in those sources as Chihiro.
This page deliberately does not publish a
surname, employer, private contact details, or identifying information outside her
public video role.

## The “Australian Guy” source trail

Chihiro's August 2018 first-person account and Daniel's March and November 2018
accounts are reconciled on the dedicated [Australian Guy
page](/wiki/characters/australian-guy/). Chihiro described a former connection and
said no later meeting occurred; Daniel's stronger later claims remain attributed to
him. His November coercive-divorce and infidelity scenarios are explicitly labelled
as fantasies, not events.

## Evidence-led timeline

### March 2018: Daniel's marriage-labelled account

The March 15 source is titled _WE GOT MARRIED_. Separate `base.en` and `small.en`
passes materially agree at **02:06–02:44** that Daniel said registration had happened
several days earlier, called himself married, acknowledged that he then had no paper
to display, and expected documentation later. At **05:51–06:14**, both passes render
him describing the paperwork and signing as complete and reporting that an official
said they were technically married.

No reviewer directly heard the audio. No civil record or independently authenticated
account from the other party was located in the bounded review, and the source does
not name its wife as Chihiro. The later application discussion and witness, family,
and immigration detail are omitted. This is Daniel's machine-rendered account of the
source's marriage framing—not proof of a legal marriage, its date, or the other
party's identity.

### 2020: a divorce-labelled record

A February 18 archive record is titled _I am now Officially Divorced_. That title
shows the creator's public framing. Separate `base.en` and `small.en` passes materially
agree at **00:01–00:30** that Daniel said he had received an image presented to him as
filed divorce paperwork. At **01:29–02:44**, both render him attributing the
relationship breakdown to spending time on Discord rather than with his wife, a lack
of common ground and a language barrier, and an inability or unwillingness to change.
At **04:05–04:17**, he is machine-rendered acknowledging uncertainty over whether the
paper meant a finalized divorce or merely that papers had been received.

That uncertainty is essential. No reviewer directly heard the audio, and no civil
record or independently authenticated account from the other party was located in the
bounded review. The account does not establish a legal divorce, its timing, the other
party's identity, or Chihiro's perspective. Cheating, court, and private-message
details from the surrounding source are deliberately excluded.

## What is intentionally omitted

The archive index contains later titles with conflicting, intimate, or accusatory
wording about Chihiro. They are not repeated here. A source title cannot by itself
establish another person's motives, private conduct, health, legal history, or current
life. The page also avoids private family details and does not use community
speculation to fill gaps.

## Page verification status

This profile is **source matched**. The archive identities and public-name title are
reconciled, and the reproduced frame plus limited cooking footage were visually
checked. The external YouTube and Instagram profiles are linked to each other, and
one public channel upload is reconciled to its copy in HIMR Archive 2. A disclosed
human visual comparison supports a public-persona connection to the 2019 HIMR
participant without asserting a legal identity. The 2018
marriage-labelled and 2020 divorce-labelled accounts are published only as narrow,
two-model machine-rendered self-accounts by Daniel. No reviewer directly heard those
passages, and neither legal status nor the unnamed wife's identity is established.
