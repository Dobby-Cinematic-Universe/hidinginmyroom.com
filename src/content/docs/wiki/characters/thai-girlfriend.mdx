“my girlfriend.” The HIMRFAM community also uses **Fast** and **Faster** for the

## Why this page says Fast and Faster

Two official Reddit embeds preserve the usage particularly clearly. An April 2, 2024
post title says *Daniel talks about the future of his relationship with
Thai-Girlfriend (Fast)*. A July 10 post title asks *Have Daniel and Faster broken up?*
This establishes both spellings as community vocabulary by those dates. It does not
authenticate a name or prove either post's relationship claims.

### 2023: travel- and livestream-labelled records

Relationship language remained recurring in the public source titles. A Phuket record
labelled May 29 is titled *Honeymoon trip with my Girlfriend in Phuket — 1 year
together ❤️*. That wording is useful evidence of the creator-supplied framing, but
it cannot supply an exact anniversary. The same raw-video
URL is attached to a second May 30 transcript record, so it counts as one media
source rather than two.

In August, a group of transcript records is titled *FIRST Stream with my
Girlfriend!* or close variants. Nine records point to one archived media URL. A
separate August 4 source title says *i accidentally leaked my girlfriend's face on
livestream...*. The title is enough to document the source framing of the reveal
as accidental; the wiki therefore does not reproduce it. Unchecked transcript
locators remain research leads only.

## Why no explanatory narrative is published

<Aside type="caution" title="Private machine-only detail withheld">
  The only preserved explanatory text for the relevant 2024 records is automated, and
  the archive maps no raw media to those transcript pages. It includes private travel,
  relationship, and financial detail about a living person. The wiki therefore does
  not summarize it as a public research lead. Candidate locators are retained only in
  the maintainers' private review queue pending media recovery and direct listening.
</Aside>

The source-matched public boundary is the four titles above: they frame a planned
Thailand departure, a US move by an unnamed girlfriend, and a broadly titled “final
chapter.” They do not establish that the participant was Faster, that any plan happened,
why Daniel left, when a relationship ended, or what Faster wanted. A later theory that
Daniel left Faster because of Sunny is likewise unsupported by these title records.

Still unresolved are the exact relationship chronology, Faster's own public account,
and which girlfriend-labelled appearances, if any, show the same person.

## What this profile does not claim

- It does not publish or choose among alleged real names.
- It does not treat Fast or Faster as a verified legal or preferred name.
- It does not assume that every generic girlfriend label identifies one person.
- It does not infer consent from a title using “accidentally leaked” wording.
- It does not establish legal marriage, an address, employment, or family details.
- It does not assign truth to Daniel's private relationship commentary merely
  because it appears in a transcript.
