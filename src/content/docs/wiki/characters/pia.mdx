“Pia” is a public nickname used in HIMR discussion. The strongest directly checked
sources are now two visual records: a 2020 creator-edited story that places a selfie
and a `pia`-labelled chat capture in the same montage, and an October 2022 stream frame
in which viewers address or discuss someone as Pia. Together they establish a public
source association and later nickname usage—not a legal identity, ownership of the
displayed account, bilateral confirmation, or proof that every “Pia” story concerns
one person.

<Aside type="caution" title="A label, not a verified biography">
  Daniel's archive contains sexually and personally sensitive, one-sided story titles.
  Machine text is not verified speech. The page records one minimized account only
  because two independent ASR passes materially agree and the same video visibly
  supplies the Pia label; it remains an unverified allegation attributed to Daniel.
  No conduct is treated as established fact about the pictured participant.
</Aside>

## The 2020 creator-edited association

The raw-backed March 2020 video *Why I Slept with my Fan* supplies a stronger bridge
than community agreement alone. At approximately 00:20–00:22, the video visibly
displays a selfie of two people. At 01:20, the same edited montage displays a captured
chat whose public labels include `pia` and `Daniel - HIMR`.

That sequencing establishes that the **published HIMR video associated the Pia label
with its story and imagery**. It does not authenticate the private chat, prove that
the pictured woman controlled the displayed account, establish consent to the
publication, or verify the sexual claim in the title. The wiki therefore includes the
source image while keeping the pictured participant's identity attributed and
qualified.

on a high-level chronology. In Daniel's account, online contact with a viewer led to
a private London meeting while he was still married. He described the meeting as
secret and later claimed that exposure of subsequent conversations contributed to
the breakdown of his marriage.

### Pia is not a synonym for “Essex Girl”

One candidate span in the May 2022 machine transcript appears to distinguish its
“Essex girl” subject from Pia. Because that distinction has not yet been directly
heard in the raw audio, the conservative rule is still the useful one: **do not merge
the two labels**. A future audio review could support a relationship between records;
it should not manufacture one now.

## Evidence ledger

- **Video-checked fact:** the 2020 montage visibly displays a selfie and, separately,
  a `pia` / `Daniel - HIMR` chat capture; this is a first-party editorial association,
  not authenticated identity or proof of the title.
- **Video-checked fact:** viewers visibly used “Pia” in the October 2022 stream chat.
- **Source-matched fact:** the four earlier title records exist, but their titles do
  not use Pia.
- **Machine-transcript account:** two passes materially agree on Daniel's minimized
  2020 chronology above; it remains unverified, one-sided, and queued for direct
  audio review. Proposed links to the other 2021–2022 narratives remain unresolved.
- **Unknown:** account ownership, whether all uses mean one person, whether the 2022
  voice is connected to the older titles, and whether any intimate story occurred as
  framed.

### Contributor-supplied Discord statement about an arcade

A contributor identifies Pia's unofficial-Discord username as `vaeleri` and supplies
the following short excerpt, timestamped **July 18, 2026 at 7:00 AM**. No timezone,
channel permalink, message IDs, or retained snapshot were supplied:

> “wym<br />
> already been talking for months”
>
> “yeah he said on stream<br />
> we went arcade”

If accurately attributed, the excerpt establishes that the `vaeleri` account said it
had been talking with Daniel for months and attributed an arcade statement to Daniel.
It does **not** independently prove that a meetup happened or that Daniel made the
stream statement. The spelling also differs: public `vae1eri` contains the digit `1`,
while the reported Discord alias `vaeleri` contains a letter `l`.

<Aside type="caution" title="No arcade passage was found in the retained August streams">
  Two-model machine searches covered all seven locally retained August 2026 public
  uploads, including about 13 hours 40 minutes of long-stream audio. They found no
  arcade wording or Pia-meetup passage. That is a negative result for those seven
  surviving sources only: the July statement could refer to an earlier, deleted,
  unlisted, or otherwise unavailable stream.
</Aside>
