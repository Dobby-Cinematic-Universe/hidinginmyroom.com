**Van Girl is Daniel's temporary public label for an unnamed contact discussed in
August 2026.** It is not a name, an identification, proof of a romantic relationship,
or a finding that the contact owned or lived in a van. This page reconciles a small
claim chronology in Daniel's exported messages with later public-stream leads.

<Aside type="caution" title="One account's messages, not a biography of the other person">
  The supplied dataset is an author-only export: it does not include the other
  participants' messages, replies, or enough conversation context to test Daniel's
  descriptions. The contributor describes the channels as public, and exact Discord
  permalinks are retained, but viewing them can still depend on Discord and server
  access. No image, voice message, handle, account ID, attachment URL, or location is
  reproduced here.
</Aside>

## Evidence boundary

- **Established at source level:** the dated texts above occur in the supplied
  export under the listed message IDs and channels; the August 26 public stream
  resolves to the listed stable video ID. Its one-pass machine text is not verified
  speech.
- **Attributed to Daniel:** the contact label, phone-number claim, proposed camping,
  plan to show places, withheld location, retrofit and offered-help account,
  meeting claims, “combining” wording, and the September decision to stop pursuing
  contact are all Daniel's one-sided account claims.
- **Not established:** Van Girl's identity, preferred label, response, residence,
  ownership of a van, messages from her, a completed trip or project, any meeting, a
  romantic or sexual relationship, or whether the August 18 unnamed contact and
  later Van Girl label concern the same person.
- **Publication limit:** no location or private media is published. See the
  [Discord Source Map](/wiki/archive/discord-source-map/) for the export's broader
  provenance and limitations.
