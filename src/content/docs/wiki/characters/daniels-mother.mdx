A participant Daniel presents as his mother appears in one visually checked HIMR
segment and is widely called **Cammie** in the community. The wiki keeps that name as
a public search label, not as a verified legal or preferred name. The archive also
contains many titles using **“mum”** or **“mummy,”** but a term scan alone does not
show an appearance or prove that every title concerns the same person.

<Aside type="caution" title="Term matches do not establish identity">
  Across the 2,227-record primary title index, 22 titles contain *mum* or
  *mummy*. That set includes commentary and at least one title referring to a
  “Japanese mum,” so it is not a 22-appearance inventory of Daniel's mother. The
  page uses Cammie only with the source limits below and does not turn it into a
  legal-name or cross-video facial-identification claim.
</Aside>

## Why this page uses “Cammie”

The generated transcript for the April 7, 2021 record _Streaming with my Mummy_
renders a participant as acknowledging that viewers got the name **Cammie** right at
about 03:31. That is the closest located HIMR role-to-name bridge, but the source text
is automatic and its audio has not been human-checked for this page.

## 2026 debt-to-mother account

In two independent offline-ASR passes over the July 2026 _why I quit YouTube_
stream, Daniel appears to say that he expected to pay his mother **£1,000** at the
end of that week. Immediately afterward, he puts **“the debt” at £22,700**. The
context likely points to his mother as the creditor, but the balance sentence does
not explicitly say that the entire £22,700 was owed to her.
