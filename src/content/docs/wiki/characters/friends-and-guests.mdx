HIMR titles name many other creators, but a named person is not automatically a guest
or collaborator. This directory separates three things that are often conflated:

1. an archive title that presents someone as appearing or meeting Daniel;
2. a commentary or response video that merely names someone; and
3. an on-camera appearance directly visible in preserved footage.

<Aside type="caution" title="Source labels, not endorsements">
  Charged words such as “problem,” “confronting,” or “stalking” are reproduced
  only when needed to identify an exact archive record. The wiki does not adopt
  the title's allegation or imply that the named person agreed to collaborate.
</Aside>

## Inclusion standard for future profiles

A person receives a dedicated profile only when there is enough public, non-invasive
material to explain their role without leaning on rumor. A new entry should record:

- the exact title and stable media identity;
- whether the person is visibly or audibly present;
- how the public name was established;
- duplicate or excerpt relationships between records; and
- which statements are Daniel's account rather than the guest's or an independent
  source's account.

Private friends, dates, bystanders, and family members are not identified merely
because viewers speculate about them.
