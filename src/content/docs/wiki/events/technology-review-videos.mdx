Technology demonstrations recur alongside the archive's
[gaming videos](/wiki/events/gaming-and-review-videos/) and
[cooking videos](/wiki/events/cooking-videos/). The selected examples span camera
unboxing, assembling a smartphone filming rig, a phone-and-desktop demonstration
and another phone unboxing. This is a history of the video formats, not buying
advice or an independent evaluation of the products.

## 2020: demonstrating the filming setup itself

The smartphone-vlogging video focuses on accessories used to present video.
At **00:00:30**, a phone, small light panel, microphone and mounting pieces are
laid out separately. At **00:04:00**, hands hold a small support while the other
parts remain nearby. By **00:09:10**, the phone, light panel and microphone
appear together in the assembled rig. “Ultimate” is the title's assessment,
not a finding about recording quality or value.
