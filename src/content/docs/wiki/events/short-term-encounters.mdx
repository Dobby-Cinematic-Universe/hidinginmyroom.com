HIMR repeatedly used first-person titles about dating, hookups, sexual experiences,
and brief encounters. This page now keeps two evidence layers separate: a
**publication-history catalogue** of video titles and a **Daniel-attributed Patreon
writing map**. It is not a list of people Daniel demonstrably met or had sex with,
and it must not be used to construct one.

<Aside

  framing, and an allegation of blackmail. The linked videos may contain
  explicit or consent-sensitive discussion. No intimate image, private identity,
  contact detail, or machine-generated sexual account is reproduced here. Legal and
  private identities remain unidentified; public Pia and Caesium role labels are
  included only under the qualified source routes stated below.
</Aside>

<Aside type="danger" title="Unverified accounts and allegations">

  or blackmail attempt.

### Japanese-man “bisexual guy” label and the received-act connection

The sources support a stronger **record connection** than the previous wording showed.
Internet Archive retains `M4PdbuE-m44` under both _My Gay Experience With A Japanese
Guy_ and _Why I let a Japanese guy give me a blowjob_. The two original MP4 objects
are byte-identical: each is 133,512,428 bytes, with MD5
`2591876f176b5eb1c7f51823e032aed2`, SHA-1
`b5e631ce6319aadddc4f07fb3a37ce5bda026f03`, and a 583.56-second duration. These are
two filenames for the same archived video, not separate encounters.

The retained Patreon writing adds three distinct connections:

- May 20 post `11282590` publicly labels the pictured private role a **Japanese
  bisexual guy**, while Daniel's locked caption claims that the man performed oral sex
  on him.
- July 25 post `13344792` repeats the received-act claim and Daniel's bisexual label
  while placing the man in the same French-former-girlfriend social cluster. The private
  photographs and appearance or family details are not reproduced.
- March 30, 2019 post `25739362` directly targets the archived `M4PdbuE-m44` file and
  says Daniel wondered what would have happened had he said yes to sex with the man.
  That counterfactual is not a denial of the separately claimed received act; the post
  does not explain what prompted it.

The recurring captioned role and the exact video/repost bridge therefore belong in
one navigation cluster, but they do not authenticate the private man as the same
person in every record or prove that every record describes one event. No stable name
or account ID is present, and the encounter date remains unknown.

The same-day `11283194` embed is adjacent evidence, not part of the exact M4 bridge. It
uses the subject _I got a blowjob (unedited)_ and YouTube ID `CbfZ3sEywC4`, but names
no participant and has not been matched to a retained public video. It is therefore
not counted as another Japanese-man event or silently merged with `M4PdbuE-m44`.
