HIMR repeatedly used first-person titles about dating, hookups, sexual experiences,
and brief encounters. This page is a **publication-history catalogue**: it records
what Daniel called those videos and whether the archive still maps them to raw media.
It is not a list of people Daniel demonstrably met or had sex with, and it must not be
used to construct one.

<Aside

  described only by nationality, gender, relationship status, or “viewer” remain
  unidentified.
</Aside>

## Audit of the circulating year-by-year list

A contributor supplied an unverified, non-exhaustive year-bucketed list of alleged
sexual partners. It was useful as a **discovery index**, but publishing it as a
body-count chronology would overstate the source record. The audit produced three
different outcomes:

- Seven additional exact or near-exact creator-title records were located and added
  to the catalogue above. The records verify publication labels, not the list's more
  graphic narratives or a one-record-to-one-person mapping.
- Recurring public labels such as Chihiro, Turkish Girl, Pia, Mila, Faster, Blood
  Bucket, Sunny, and Weed Girl already have source-qualified pages. Those pages
  document different kinds of public association; the supplied list is not enough to
  reclassify every person as a sexual partner.
- Claims found only as private-place stories, nationality or body descriptors,
  Discord recollections, medical labels, or anonymous community lore remain omitted.
  They need a public source, exact locator, direct review, and privacy-sensitive
  wording before they could be considered.

The year buckets are also not event dates. Most located dates are archive labels for
when Daniel published or streamed a retelling, sometimes years after the alleged
event. Duplicate titles, retrospectives, and euphemisms make a defensible partner
count impossible from the current evidence.

## 2020–2023: retellings, euphemism, and the recovered October record

The 2017 and 2020 “Gay Experience” titles may look like two entries for the same
story, but title similarity is not enough to merge them. Conversely, it is not enough
to count them as two distinct events. Both remain separate source records until the
audio is checked. The title labels also do not determine Daniel's sexual orientation.

The May 2020 record is cross-referenced on the [“cereal” terminology

## The misleading June 2017 “10 year old boy” title

One June 9, 2017 archive label reads _I slept with a 10 year old boy_. It is **not an
adult encounter record and must not be read as a sexual-abuse allegation**.

The snapshot preserves three title records for the same media identity:

| Archive title                    | Platform-ID candidate | Media duration | File hashes                     |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------- | -------------: | ------------------------------- |
| _I slept with a 10 year old boy_ | `q5UkyKkBioA`         | 363.51 seconds | Identical MD5, SHA-1, and CRC32 |
| _I slept with my best friend_    | `q5UkyKkBioA`         | 363.51 seconds | Identical MD5, SHA-1, and CRC32 |
| _My first sleepover_             | `q5UkyKkBioA`         | 363.51 seconds | Identical MD5, SHA-1, and CRC32 |

Thus, the charged wording is an alternate catalogue title for the same file that is
also labelled as a best-friend childhood sleepover. In English, “slept with” can mean
either sharing sleep or having sex; the sibling records resolve how this archive item
must be classified. Nothing in this metadata supports a sexual act, abuse, or an
adult–minor encounter. The record is excluded from the ten-item adult chronology.

## Verification boundary

This page verifies **records and mappings**, not intimate history. A future human
review should check the queued raw-audio spans for exact wording, speaker continuity,
whether a title accurately summarizes the video, and whether later videos retell an
older account. Any publishable expansion must remain attributed to Daniel, avoid
identifying private participants, preserve uncertainty about consent and legality,
and include a response and corroboration status for any wrongdoing allegation.
