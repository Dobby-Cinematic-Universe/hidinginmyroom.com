Drawing appears in several forms across the archive: serialized webcomics,
character-design experiments, timelapses, memory portraits, a painting tutorial
and live paper drawing. The examples below distinguish a project's own description
from what a video title, machine transcript or inspected frame can establish.
Fictional characters and remembered portraits are not documentary records of the
people or events they may evoke.

## Early webcomics: Hazel’s Planet of Ice and The Shop Lifter

The creator-linked [ComicFury profile](/wiki/overview/daniel-lord/#historical-comicfury-self-link)
leads to projects beyond *Another Boring Night Shift*. *Hazel’s Planet of Ice* is
listed as science fiction: its description introduces a space setting and an
investigation after a vessel crashes. The accompanying author note leaves
continuation uncertain after a break and says the drawing style has changed.
ComicFury displays **10 August 2016** as its last-update label; that is not a
creation date or evidence of a completed story.
