## Butt, armpit, and natural body scent

A hash-pinned full-file offline-ASR pass of the retained August 1, 2017 video renders
Daniel answering a question about fetishes and describing a strong interest in
**natural human body odor**. In self-description segments at approximately
**00:00:12.72–00:00:24.36** and **00:02:05.64–00:02:34.40**, it associates arousal
with butt odor, sweat, body oils, and scent before showering. It also renders him
saying that smell is an important part of sex for him, that a person with no body
odor feels less human to him, and that he prefers pre-shower to post-shower sex.

The pass renders him calling his attraction to Asian women an “obvious” fetish near
**00:00:12–00:00:19**, then adds **armpit odor** to the same scent pattern around
**00:02:44–00:02:56** (machine segment start **00:02:44.24**). That is Daniel's
objectifying self-description, not the wiki's
classification of Asian women or a factual claim about any former partner.

His own qualification matters. Around **00:03:28.56–00:03:44**, the pass renders him
saying that the channel was becoming “weird,” that he had woken only minutes earlier,
and that viewers should forget anything strange he said. This is contextual framing,
not a clear retraction of the preceding description. The video then changes into a
cooking/outro sequence; repeated words detected there are not treated as part of his
to-camera self-description.
