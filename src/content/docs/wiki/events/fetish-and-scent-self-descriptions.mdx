## Butt, armpit, and natural body scent

Independent offline-ASR review of the August 1, 2017 raw video renders Daniel answering a question about
fetishes and describing a strong interest in **natural human body odor**. In the
opening minutes, it repeatedly associates arousal with butt odor,
sweat, body oils, and partners' scent before showering. It also renders him saying
that scent is an important part of sex for him and that he prefers natural odor to a
freshly washed, scentless presentation.

The later part of the transcript adds **armpit odor** to the same scent pattern. It
also renders him calling his attraction to Asian women an “obvious” fetish. That is
Daniel's objectifying self-description, not the wiki's classification of Asian women
or of any named former partner.

## 2020: “smell fan” and chat-supplied “olfactophile”

The upstream archive index incorrectly points the six-hour *Self Isolation — Day 1-2*
record to the 38-minute Part 1 file. Internet Archive also holds a Part 2-labelled
original lasting **06:05:22.03**, within 4.03 seconds of the transcript's displayed
**06:05:18**. The normalized manifest now uses that corrected file.

Independent offline-ASR review of the corrected raw source renders Daniel saying his
interest had shifted away from underwear sniffing toward armpit hair around
**05:52:08–05:52:25**. Around **05:54:28–05:55:36**, it renders him calling himself a
“smell fan” or “smell lover” and adopting **“olfactophile”** after chat supplies the
word. This is useful as Daniel's own public label, not as a clinical diagnosis.

The same passage also matters because at **05:53:54–05:54:08** it renders Daniel
explicitly saying he was **not into feet**, then describing an interest in scalp scent.
That makes the later 2024 foot-odor machine lead an inconsistency, not simple
corroboration.

interest in natural body scent—especially butt and armpit odor—and separately said he
enjoyed sniffing used underwear. In 2020 he adopted “olfactophile” after chat supplied
