Gaming is a recurring production format in the HIMR archive, not merely an
occasional subject of conversation. The examples below span **Elden Ring**, **Diablo
4**, **Helldivers 2** and **Path of Exile 2**: build guides, a weapon-focused test and
a comparative review. Their titles establish how the recordings were presented;
sampled raw frames add limited visual context. This is a selected sequence, not a
complete catalogue or a count of all gaming uploads.

For making games rather than reviewing or playing them, see the separate
[game-development videos](/wiki/events/game-development-videos/). The
[technology-review article](/wiki/events/technology-review-videos/) covers
hardware demonstrations rather than game verdicts.

This page concerns the videos themselves. Channel names and platform history remain
on [Channel Bans & Platforms](/wiki/events/channel-bans-and-platforms/), while
[Work & Income](/wiki/events/work-and-income-claims/) covers attributed earnings and work
claims. Game balance, prices and promotional offers below are historical source
content, not current advice or endorsements.
