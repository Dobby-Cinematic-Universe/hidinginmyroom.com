ASMR-labelled uploads span several formats in the archive: cooking instructions,
eating sounds, an ambient train journey and bedtime reading. These examples extend
beyond the cat-purring title already listed in the
[2015 source catalogue](/wiki/archive/2015-source-catalog/).
They belong alongside the [cooking videos](/wiki/events/cooking-videos/), but are
not all food videos or conventional spoken vlogs.

The titles describe how each recording was presented. In particular, “ASMR” and
“No Talking” do not establish a relaxation effect or verify that every part of a
soundtrack contains no speech.

## Eating sounds and ambient travel in 2019

The **23 June 2019** filename-labelled *ASMR JAPANESE OMELETTE AND KETCHUP RICE
MUKBANG* includes both “No Talking” and “EATING SOUNDS” in its title. This is a
useful example of food-focused presentation even though the corpus has no admitted
transcript for it. The title remains a catalogue description, not a verified account
of the meal or the complete soundtrack.
