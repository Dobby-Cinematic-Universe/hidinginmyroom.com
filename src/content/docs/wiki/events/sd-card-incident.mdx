“The SD card incident” is used here as a navigation label for a group of public 2019
records. The strongest surviving item is the March 19 upload titled _What’s on the SD
card_. Nearby archive records carry marriage-over and divorce wording, while an
earlier February _Breakfast with Daniel_ record is a machine-transcript candidate.

The wife in this period is generally known in the HIMR community as
[Chihiro](/wiki/characters/chihiro/), and this page follows that convention. The
SD-card clips themselves use the generic label **wife** rather than audibly or visibly
naming Chihiro, so source-specific citations retain that limitation.

That source trail makes the topic notable enough to index. It does **not** prove what
was on a card, that the four records form one uninterrupted account, that a card caused
a separation, or that a civil divorce followed on the timetable suggested by titles.

<Aside type="danger" title="Sensitive account not yet audio verified">
  Archive machine text covers the earlier records, and two independent offline
  speech-recognition passes closely agree on the opening five minutes of the
  March 19 source. The detailed section below is a high-level summary of
  **Daniel's alleged account**, not verified speech or a finding about Chihiro
  or any unnamed person. No SD card, underlying file, third-party statement,
  consent record, age evidence, legal record, or independent corroboration was
  reviewed. A later age-related passage is deliberately omitted.
</Aside>

## What the March 19 raw file visibly establishes

The transcript page displays **00:05:01**, while the reconciled raw file runs
**00:12:36.78**. Sampling across the complete picture and a denser check around the
edit show Daniel speaking alone to camera through the opening section. Around
**00:05:05.5**, the video hard-cuts into a separate outdoor bushcraft/cooking segment.
That explains the large duration mismatch without treating the later footage as more
SD-card testimony.

One substantive editorial element can be checked without accepting the soundtrack:
from approximately **00:01:11–00:01:17**, a creator-added overlay visibly reads,
“This skype call took place around may 2018.” That verifies how Daniel dated a call
in his published edit. It does not independently establish that a call occurred, its
participants or contents, or the accuracy of the displayed month and year.

## Daniel's unverified account

### How he claimed the card was found

The February 22 machine transcript renders Daniel saying that he left an SD card in
Japan while returning to England because of his father's condition. In that account,
Chihiro later returned to Japan, found the card, and looked through it. The March 6
machine transcript renders him adding that she sent him screenshots of what she had
found and that they had gone roughly two weeks without speaking.

The March 17 machine transcript then renders Daniel saying that Chihiro had lost trust
after seeing the card and wanted a divorce. That is his claimed chronology and causal
interpretation. It is not Chihiro's authenticated account, proof that the card was the
sole cause, or a civil-status record.

### What Daniel claimed was on it

Independent `base.en` and `small.en` passes agree on the main structure of Daniel's
March 19 account. They render him describing:

- multiple screenshots from sexually explicit Skype video calls with an unnamed
  person; the checked creator overlay dates one call to around May 2018;
- an approximately seven-second post-hotel clip that he dated to November or December
  2017, which he claimed did not record sexual activity or identify the unnamed person
  beyond clothed legs; and
- other intimate images he said had been sent to him by unnamed people.

The machine text also renders Daniel arguing that the hotel clip predated the
marriage and giving his own view of whether that made it adultery under Japanese law.
The wiki does not adopt that legal interpretation. It also does not infer permission
to retain or show any material, because no underlying file, sender, participant,
consent record, or applicable legal authority was reviewed.

## Identity and causation boundaries

- This page follows the established community identification of Daniel's wife as
  Chihiro. The SD-card sources themselves still use only **wife**, so they are not the
  source that establishes her public name or a bridge to the current Chihiro K
  profiles.
- Daniel's reported interpretation of what counted as infidelity or a legal wrong is
  not adopted. No legal authority or civil-status record was reviewed.
- The surviving source does not let the wiki inspect the card or underlying files.
  Claims about their contents remain Daniel's unverified account even after the
  audio is eventually checked.

A human reviewer should listen to the first **00:05:05** of the March 19 raw file and

above record Daniel's unverified claims, not established facts.
