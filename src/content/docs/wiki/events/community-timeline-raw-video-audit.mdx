This is an audit of the [community timeline used for
discovery](https://www.reddit.com/r/HIMRFAM/comments/1psodbp/quick_himr_timeline_from_1500_transcripts/),
not an endorsement of it. Reddit supplied search terms. The conclusions below come
from the public raw-video archive, direct frame checks, complete reviewed subreddit
media, archive metadata, and the bounded ASR reviews documented on the linked pages.

<Aside type="caution" title="Disposition labels">
  **Raw-backed** means a preserved Internet Archive video supports at least a source
  title, visible action, or warned creator self-account. **Community-media checked**
  means the complete surviving repost and its post context were reviewed; it does not
  authenticate a missing original. **Partial** means only the narrow linked statement
  is publishable. **Withheld** means the timeline adds a person, motive, diagnosis,
  location, amount, legal status, or allegation that the reviewed sources do not
  establish.

To avoid republishing unsupported lore, multiple withheld names and allegations
are grouped below rather than itemized. Their omission is the audit result.

</Aside>
