OnlyFans became a recurring subject in HIMR titles between May 2020 and May 2022.
This page is complete for records whose **archive title contains “OnlyFans”**
(case-insensitive) in the August 25, 2026 snapshot. It is not a count of every
passing mention inside unrelated videos.

  images, leaked or paywalled material, adult-profile links, or identifying details
  about private third parties. The linked HIMR videos may contain frank discussion;
  use discretion when opening them.
</Aside>

earnings and success framing in 2020, through consumer/platform criticism and claimed
personal costs, to a one-year earnings retrospective and policy alarm in 2021, then
an intended-exit label in May 2022. The labels sometimes pull in other creators or
unnamed private people, but this page neither identifies those private people nor
adopts Daniel's descriptions of them.

## What remains unverified

- whether Daniel owned or controlled the account discussed in any video;
- subscriber, gross-income, net-income, fee, tax, balance, or payout figures;
- whether any platform restriction, ban, or policy change applied to his account;
- whether the title-labelled “one year” period or intended exit matched later account
  activity;
- causal claims involving dating, family, housing, harassment, or career effects; and
- descriptions of another person's profile or content.

The archive transcripts and NotebookLM drafts remain useful search aids, but their
speech-derived wording is not evidence on this page. A future audio review should
listen to the raw files, preserve precise timestamps and surrounding context, and
record Daniel's statements as attributed self-reports—not convert them into facts.
