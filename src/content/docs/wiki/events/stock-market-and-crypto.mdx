The archive preserves a concentrated run of stock-market and cryptocurrency uploads
from October 2020 through May 2023. This page keeps three evidence layers separate:

1. **Archive titles** are first-party publication labels, not findings by the wiki.
2. **Daniel's financial claims** remain unverified unless a human has listened to the
   raw audio and the claim is still attributed to him.
3. **Visible facts** are limited to what can be seen at the cited raw-video frames.

<Aside type="danger" title="No financial result is independently verified">
  No brokerage statement, bank record, tax return, wallet address, exchange record,
  or trade confirmation was matched to this sequence. Every personal amount, return,
  holding, or loss appearing in an archive title is Daniel's uncorroborated
  first-person framing—not a verified balance or result.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Not investment advice">
  These historical titles and graphics document the channel. They are not
  recommendations, and this page does not assess whether any security, strategy, or
  cryptocurrency is suitable or likely to rise or fall.
</Aside>

## Accuracy rules for future additions

- Do not combine figures across uploads unless the accounts, date ranges, deposits,
  withdrawals, currencies, and realized-versus-unrealized status are known.
- Preserve qualifiers such as “about,” “roughly,” and “I think.”
- A screenshot can establish displayed content, but not ownership, authenticity,
  completeness, or the absence of other accounts.
- Keep predictions as historical channel content, never as findings by this wiki.
