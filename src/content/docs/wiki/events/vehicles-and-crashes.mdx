This chronology keeps three kinds of evidence separate:

1. an **archive title**, which is the source's label rather than a factual finding;
2. a **first-person claim**, which must remain attributed to Daniel; and
3. a **visible fact**, limited to what appears in the cited raw-video frames.

<Aside type="danger" title="No finding of fault, offence, ownership, or injury">
  The archive is not a police report, insurance file, medical record, vehicle
  registration, purchase contract, or official driving record. This page does not
  decide who caused an incident, whether an offence occurred, whether anyone was
  injured, or who legally owned or financed a vehicle.
</Aside>

## Accuracy rules for future additions

- Do not convert a dramatic title into a finding of fact.
- Attribute off-camera events and test results to the speaker unless an official
  record is available and appropriate to publish.
- Describe only what a checked frame or continuous segment visibly shows.
- Never infer cause, fault, illegality, damage, or injury from a vehicle's position or
  from a narrator's reaction alone.
- A vehicle appearing in a video does not establish ownership, payment, or financing.
