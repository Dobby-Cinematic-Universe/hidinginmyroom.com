**“Cereal” demonstrably functioned as a substitute for sexual terminology in at
least two author-written HIMR contexts.** Daniel says so directly in retained 2018
and 2019 Patreon bodies. That does not create a universal search-and-replace rule:
the archive also contains literal food contexts, the exact 2017 spoken form remains
unheard, and each story or later callback still needs its own evidence.

<Aside type="caution" title="A contextual definition—not proof of any story">
  Daniel's written explanation establishes how he says he used the word in two
  posts. It does not verify an encounter, identify an unnamed partner, prove that
  platform systems were evaded, or establish that every use of cereal is sexual.
  The earlier spoken-origin material remains machine-only unless explicitly stated
  otherwise below.
</Aside>

## Strongest located origin candidate

The strongest located machine-text candidate is the October 14, 2017 *5k Subscriber Celebration Live
Stream*. Its machine transcript places a substitution discussion near 00:35:15,
an announcement of future usage near 00:35:35, and later age-restriction or
monetization context near 00:37:30 and 00:43:40.

That sequence explains why researchers focus on this stream, but it is not enough to
define the term or quote any line. The raw 2:38:48 video is mapped and available; the

### 2018–2019: Daniel's written explanation

Two access-controlled Patreon bodies remove the ambiguity for their own contexts:

- In [_Age restricted and sex
  videos_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/age-restricted-18735121)
  (May 11, 2018; post `18735121`, **L**), Daniel says he substituted **cereal** for
  sexual terminology because he expected that wording to reduce the chance of an age
  restriction. He then says the video was restricted anyway. This is Daniel's account
  of his wording and rationale—not a YouTube enforcement record or proof of why the
  platform acted.
- In [_Life
  update_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/life-update-28047708)
  (July 1, 2019; post `28047708`, **L**), Daniel again describes cereal and other
  substitute words as a way he believed he could discuss sexual subjects while
  retaining monetization. The statement confirms continued creator-intended coded
  use; it does not establish that every video stayed monetized or that the code caused
  any platform outcome.

The bodies are paraphrased and not redistributed. They establish a creator-written
definition in these two instances while leaving the first coinage, exact 2017 sound,
and literal-versus-coded status of other occurrences open.

## How to apply the contextual reading

Researchers may use the coded reading where Daniel explicitly defines it or the
surrounding source independently establishes that context, while keeping the
literal-food reading open elsewhere. A mechanical search-and-replace rule would
create false claims.

| Evidence class | What it supports |
| --- | --- |
| Archive title or raw filename | The creator publicly used that label. |
| Access-controlled Patreon explanation | Daniel's intended meaning and claimed platform rationale in that exact written context; not a universal definition or platform finding. |
| Machine-transcript context | A locator and interpretation to check, not a quotation. |
| Same-account direct-text pairing | Contextual use in that exchange, not proof of the story or a universal definition. |
| Repeated contextual use | A working interpretation to test, with uncertainty stated. |
| Daniel's coded story | Daniel's account at most; never automatic proof about a partner. |

The concise glossary note is therefore: **a creator-confirmed substitute for sexual
terminology in at least two written Patreon contexts; other occurrences remain
context-dependent, and the exact 2017 spoken origin still requires direct audio
review.**
