This is a chronology of **source language**, not a list of verified relationships.
The archive contains many titles using words such as “girlfriend,” “wife,” “married,”
and “divorced.” Those labels establish how an upload was presented; they do not by
themselves identify the other person, prove a legal status, or establish that two
nearby records concern the same person.

Brief dating and sexual-story titles are catalogued separately on [Short-Term
Encounters](/wiki/events/short-term-encounters/). That separation prevents an unnamed
one-video account from being silently attached to a recurring name on this page.

<Aside type="caution" title="How to read this page">
  A title-level entry means only that a preserved record exists under that label.
  Spoken claims are accepted as verified only when the raw audio is manually
  reviewed. This router identifies a few dedicated-page machine-text research leads
  so readers can find them, but labels them unverified and does not use them as
  evidence that a relationship event happened.
</Aside>

### 2018–2020: marriage and divorce labels

A March 2018 record is titled *WE GOT MARRIED*. A February 2020 record is titled *I
am now Officially Divorced*. The two labels document the creator's public packaging,
not a civil marriage, a civil divorce, either event date, or the identity and account
of another party. The narrower source trail around the public name Chihiro is covered
on the [Chihiro profile](/wiki/characters/chihiro/).

The archive also preserves a February–March 2019 candidate sequence ending with an
item explicitly titled *What s on the SD card*. Its [dedicated source-boundary
page](/wiki/events/sd-card-incident/) reconciles the four records, the March 19
duration mismatch, a checked creator-added dating caption, and the raw video's edit
into unrelated outdoor footage. The underlying private account remains unverified
because its audio has not been directly heard. The wife is generally known in the
community as Chihiro, although these titles themselves do not name her; the wiki does

### 2019: Turkish-Girl-labelled sequence

The archive also contains a 2019 cluster whose titles use **Turkish Girl**. Three
April 24 catalogue rows resolve to one raw-video identity, so they cannot be counted
as three meetings or three people. The dedicated [Turkish Girl
page](/wiki/characters/turkish-girl/) reconciles that duplication, a neutral checked
frame, and later title records. It keeps Daniel's spoken and intimate retellings as
unverified machine-transcript leads rather than relationship facts.

“Turkish Girl” is creator/archive wording used to navigate a source cluster. It is
not a verified nationality, legal identity, or permission to identify the unnamed
participant.

### 2020: Mila-named records

Three 2020 titles contain the name Mila: *Live Q&A with Mila*, *My
problem with Mila (The Dutch girl)*, and *Christmas with Daniel and Mila 2020*. The
first source visibly shows two people participating together on camera. Shared title
wording does **not** establish that the other two records refer to that same
participant; a visual spot-check of the Christmas video did not supply such a bridge.
The titles also do not establish cohabitation, nationality, or a particular legal or
romantic status. See the [Mila profile](/wiki/characters/mila/) for the deliberately
narrow record.

The separate [Mila Slap page](/wiki/events/mila-slap/) distinguishes two noteworthy
moments. The March Q&A visibly records donation-prompted open-hand face contact after
a machine-rendered statement that it would not be hard. The separate April argument
is off-camera but strongly source matched by aligned participant statements in the
full stream and creator-uploaded retrospective; its audio has not been manually
verified and intent remains unresolved. Broader claims about **Mila's frustration**, private
conversation, emotion, or motive remain unresolved. The bad upstream Part 2 link has
been reconciled to the full original in Internet Archive metadata, but source recovery
does not authenticate the machine transcript or establish those broader claims.

### Late 2025: Weed Girl / “Weedy” remains a community label

**Weed Girl**, **Weed Girlfriend**, and the search alias **Weedy** come from later
community usage around an unnamed participant. They do not occur as exact title
matches in the audited 2,227-record first-party catalogue or the dated 24-card
current-channel inventory. A full visual review of one Reddit-hosted repost shows
Daniel alone; its soundtrack was not directly heard and the woman does not appear.

The [Weed Girl / “Weedy” page](/wiki/characters/weed-girl/) therefore keeps
“girlfriend” as one-sided post-title framing. It also warns a title-level dating-profile
allegation without adopting it. The [2026 return page](/wiki/events/2026-return-and-car-camping/)
publishes no private-partner identity, and nothing reviewed connects the return to
Weedy, Faster, Sunny, Mila, Pia, or anyone else here.

## Evidence tiers used across the chronology

| Layer | Examples here | What it can support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Archive or platform title | Generic girlfriend labels, marriage/divorce wording, Sunny-named titles | That a source was published or indexed under that wording—not the underlying event |
| Checked visual | Mila's Q&A appearance, Pia chat usage, a masked Thailand-era appearance | Only what the cited frame or reviewed video visibly contains |
| Community title or repost | Fast/Faster, Loggy, Weed Girl/Weedy | Public community vocabulary and narrow observations from a vetted copy—not private identity or off-camera conduct |
| Machine transcript or automatic captions | Mila-frustration, the current “Spanish girl” lead, leaving-Thailand, and 2026-return leads | A locator for human review; not verified speech or relationship history |

## What this chronology omits

The archive also contains many generic dating titles, intimate claims, accusations,
and later retellings. This page does not reproduce them merely because they are
searchable. It also avoids assigning generic “girlfriend” or “wife” records to
Chihiro, the Turkish participant, Mila, the current “Spanish girl” lead,
Pia, Faster, Sunny, Crypt, or Weedy without an explicit source bridge. That keeps the
chronology useful without turning upload packaging or community shorthand into
private biography.
