incident. The preserved videos include a long kitchen livestream, a pie-making
demonstration and a later budget-food presentation. The dated demonstrations below
describe sampled raw-video frames; the meal-ration and crumble sections are
explicitly unreviewed transcript leads. The separate
[platform history](/wiki/events/channel-bans-and-platforms/#cooking-with-daniel-confirmed-label-unconfirmed-account-bridge)
retains the limits on treating the label as an authenticated standalone channel.
