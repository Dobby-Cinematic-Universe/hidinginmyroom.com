Daniel has described many ways of making money: ordinary service work, YouTube and
related creator products, adult subscriptions, online selling, investing, and app-based
delivery. This page is a map of those **public claims and source records**, not a net-worth
estimate or a reconstructed tax return.

<Aside
  type="danger"
  title="No personal financial result is independently audited"
>
  No bank statement, payslip, tax return, creator-platform payout export,
  brokerage statement, delivery-platform statement, or complete business ledger
  was matched to this article. Amounts and outcomes in titles or generated
  transcripts are Daniel's framing at most. Revenue is not the same as profit,
  an account screen is not proof of ownership, and an investment changing value
  is not necessarily realized income.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Three evidence levels are kept separate">
  **Source fact** means a dated record or visible frame was checked. **Daniel's
  account** means first-person wording that still needs attribution and, for
  speech, a human audio pass. **Generated-text lead** means an automatic
  transcript suggests a detail but the wiki has not accepted the exact words.
  Platform titles such as “banned” are never treated as notices from the named
  company.
</Aside>

### 2016: a three-part webcomic funnel

Two Patreon posts show how Daniel intended one creative project to move between
platforms. In [_drawing
timelapse!_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/drawing-6905765)
(October 2; `6905765`, **L**), he said the drawing process for a new webcomic would
go to YouTube while the finished page, text, and story would be a Patreon reward. In
[_Another boring night shift — Page
1_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/another-boring-1-6926651)
(October 4; `6926651`, **P**), he made the first page public, proposed putting later
pages behind a **$2** Patreon tier, and offered a free ComicFury mirror with ads and
less author commentary or interaction.

This is unusually clear evidence of the intended funnel—**YouTube for process,
Patreon for an enhanced reading experience, ComicFury for an ad-supported
free mirror**—but not of subscriptions, ad revenue, readership, or completion. The
second post also calls the story inspired by a recent night-shift job and past events;
that does not make its characters or incidents documentary records.
