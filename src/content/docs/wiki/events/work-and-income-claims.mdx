related creator products, adult subscriptions, online selling, investing, and app-based
delivery. This page is a map of those **public claims and source records**, not a net-worth
estimate or a reconstructed tax return.

<Aside type="danger" title="No personal financial result is independently audited">
  No bank statement, payslip, tax return, creator-platform payout export, brokerage
  statement, delivery-platform statement, or complete business ledger was matched to
  this article. Amounts and outcomes in titles or generated transcripts are Daniel's
  framing at most. Revenue is not the same as profit, an account screen is not proof of
  ownership, and an investment changing value is not necessarily realized income.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Three evidence levels are kept separate">
  **Source fact** means a dated record or visible frame was checked. **Daniel's account**
  means first-person wording that still needs attribution and, for speech, a human audio
  pass. **Generated-text lead** means an automatic transcript suggests a detail but the
  wiki has not accepted the exact words. Platform titles such as “banned” are never
  treated as notices from the named company.
</Aside>

### Investing and trading are not automatically income

Daniel's dividend, day-trading, stock, and cryptocurrency titles are catalogued on
[Markets, Crypto & Investing Claims](/wiki/events/stock-market-and-crypto/). That page
preserves dramatic gains, losses, balances, and predictions as title wording only.

For this income overview, four distinctions matter:

- a portfolio's displayed value is not realized income;
- deposits are not gains;
- gross proceeds are not profit; and
- dividends, trading gains, and losses need dates, currencies, fees, taxes, and account
  ownership before they can be combined.

The 2021 “seven sources” generated text treats dividends and stock gains as separate
categories. That proposed distinction is plausible, but the actual figures and whether
either paid living expenses remain unverified.
