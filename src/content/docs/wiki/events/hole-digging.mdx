This page uses **“The Hole”** as shorthand for a June 2020 HIMR source trail. The
primary archive contains a record labelled *Digging a hole — part 1* and maps it to
an Internet Archive MP4. That establishes a creator-titled digging video, not every
detail later attached to the episode.

## What the surviving source directly supports

Nine frames were checked directly against the mapped raw MP4 at **00:01, 01:00,
05:00, 10:00, 20:00, 40:00, 60:00, 75:00, and 80:20**. The opening sample shows a
shovel at a disturbed patch in a grassy outdoor setting. Subsequent samples show an
on-camera participant digging, a growing mound of displaced soil, and the participant
lower relative to ground level in some later samples.

Two reader-inspectable Internet Archive derivatives—the
[earlier still](https://archive.org/download/69999/69999.thumbs%2F20200612-Digging%20a%20hole%20-%20part%201-Bkff_ETOLdI_000001.jpg)
and [later still](https://archive.org/download/69999/69999.thumbs%2F20200612-Digging%20a%20hole%20-%20part%201-Bkff_ETOLdI_004820.jpg)—show
the same general setting. The source title supplies the digging context. Neither the
sampled raw frames nor the derived stills independently identify the participant or
establish an exact date, location, depth, purpose, or number of days spent digging.

## Two raw-backed June follow-ups

Two later June files contain follow-up footage even though their titles do not name
the hole. *How Heartbreak triggered my Spiritual Awakening* is a mixed-topic video:
the bounded machine passes place its hole material at approximately **00:06:55–
00:11:10**. *REALLY GREAT NEWS GUYS :D* opens inside the excavation and returns to
the project during approximately **00:00–00:04:45**.

Separate `base.en` and `small.en` passes materially agree on a limited, benign
creator account:

- In the June 20 file, Daniel says two frogs were in the hole and describes trying to
  remove them. He later calls the footage another update, says it is a few days later,
  demonstrates a step cut into the side, and says he wants to widen and deepen it.
- In the June 30 file, he says he has added steps and a seat. He estimates the hole as
  almost as deep as he is tall and gives a roughly **1.74-metre** figure with an
  explicit uncertainty qualifier in both machine renderings. That is his estimate,
  not a checked measurement and not evidence for the community's exact “six-foot”
  wording.
- He says he wants to continue after travel and describes an unrealized plan to widen
  it into a room-like space with a seat and small wall recesses or hooks. The source
  does not establish that those additions were completed.

## A significant duration mismatch

The transcript snapshot displays **00:19:11** for this record, while Internet
Archive's metadata reports **5,071.66 seconds**—approximately **01:24:31.66**—for
the mapped MP4. The wiki therefore does not assume that the transcript covers the
whole raw file or use its generated summary as a complete account.

<Aside type="caution" title="Details not promoted from the community timeline">
  A supplied [community timeline](https://www.reddit.com/r/HIMRFAM/comments/1psodbp/quick_himr_timeline_from_1500_transcripts/)
  describes a multi-day project, gives an exact depth, and proposes an emotional
  cause. Those details were not established by the title, the two checked stills, or
  a complete review of the unusually long mapped file, so they are not repeated here
  as facts or as Daniel's verified account.
</Aside>

## What remains open

A complete review of the 01:24:31 mapped file is needed before the wiki can describe
the continuous progression. Direct listening is still needed before the June 20 and
June 30 machine-rendered account can be promoted beyond a warned research lead. No
checked source supplies an instrument measurement, a complete project chronology, a
verified emotional cause, or evidence that the proposed room-like additions were
completed.
