catalogue, not a claim that a new channel was created. On August 25, 2026, the handle
[@hidinginmyroom1111](https://www.youtube.com/@hidinginmyroom1111) still resolved to
the old, stable channel ID **UC_yIF-9jOge6nNA0z-ScrBQ**. The captured catalogue

That sequence proves that material was visible again. It does **not** prove the exact
start or duration of an absence: private, unlisted, deleted, renamed, members-only,
or undiscovered material would not necessarily appear in a signed-out catalogue.
