stronger at establishing that he published debt-themed titles than at establishing
what he owed, to whom, under what terms, or whether he repaid it.

<Aside type="caution" title="Self-reported finances, not audited accounts">
  Every balance or repayment detail on this page is Daniel's attributed account. No

  confirmation was reviewed. Figures should not be added together unless the source
  clearly says they are separate and measured at the same date.

## July 2026 account

The member-gated [*why I quit
YouTube*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rKdcv4QvGig) stream contains a later
machine-speech account of current debt and repayment. The exact, cross-checked figures
and the separate amount Daniel attributes to his mother are documented below.

### Planned payment to his mother and the £22,700 balance

At **01:44:47–01:44:55**, both ASR models render Daniel saying that he expected to
pay his mother **£1,000** at the end of that week. Immediately afterward, at
**01:44:55–01:44:57**, they render him putting **“the debt” at £22,700**.

The proximity makes his mother—the person this wiki covers under the public
[“Cammie” label](/wiki/characters/daniels-mother/)—the likely creditor in context.
But the balance sentence itself does not repeat “my mother.” The precise statement is
therefore: **Daniel appears to say he planned a £1,000 payment to his mother and then
stated a £22,700 debt balance; it is likely, but not explicit, that the entire balance
was mother debt.**

<Aside type="danger" title="Do not flatten likely context into a confirmed £22,700 family loan">
  No payment, balance, creditor, or loan term was independently verified. “End of the
  week” is retained as Daniel's relative timing rather than converted into a calendar
  deadline, and nothing here supports an inference about his mother's finances.
</Aside>

### Recent-charge explanation and arithmetic mismatch

Daniel's machine-rendered explanation says the balance had not fallen because of
recent charges. Both models agree on three figures:

| Daniel's attributed category | Machine-rendered amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Council-related charge | £350 |
| Tyre expense | £240 |
| Browser-extension charge | £150 |
| **Listed total** | **£740** |

He then characterizes the setback as roughly **£600**. The page does not silently
reconcile £740 with £600: he may have rounded, meant a subtotal, misstated a figure,
or been referring to something else. None of the charges or their effect on a balance
was independently checked. His surrounding criticism of the council and bank is
omitted, and the extension charge is not labelled fraudulent as an objective fact.

### Repayment-before-travel intention

At **01:45:42–01:45:57**, the ASR renders Daniel saying that travel would wait until
the following year or until the debt was paid, and that he wanted to avoid taking on
more debt before repaying his mother. That is a stated intention at the stream date,
not a binding plan. Later travel would not by itself prove that repayment occurred—or
that the earlier account was false.
