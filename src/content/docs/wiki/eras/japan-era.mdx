“The Japan era” is editorial shorthand for several Japan-centred runs of HIMR
material, not a claim that Daniel lived in Japan continuously. The archive documents
an early 2016 vlog sequence, another sequence beginning in September 2017, domestic
and relationship-labelled material during 2018–2019, and later Japan-labelled videos.
Dates on this page are
**archive record labels** unless a sentence explicitly says otherwise.

<Aside type="caution" title="Titles and first-person accounts">
  A title proves how an archived upload was labelled, not that every statement
  in it is true. Marriage, residence, and divorce wording below remains
  source-title language; no independent civil or immigration record has been
  reviewed.
</Aside>

## Chronology

### 2016: the first archived Japan vlog run

One clearly Japan-labelled sequence in the preserved 2016 index includes
_Thai festival Yoyogi park — Japan Vlog day 2_ on May 15. The linked raw video is
present in Internet Archive item `69999`. A July 29 record is titled _leaving japan
forever,_ but that label is not treated as proof of a permanent move. Together, the
titles establish a Japan-centred run in the archive; they do not establish exact
arrival, residency, or departure dates.

### July 2016: a radiation-themed creator title

The same run contains a July 1 record titled _Japan's radiation problem — They lied
to us_. This establishes that Daniel published a radiation-themed first-person
commentary, not that he was exposed to radiation, had a particular medical condition,
or that an unnamed party lied.

A supplied [community timeline](https://www.reddit.com/r/HIMRFAM/comments/1psodbp/quick_himr_timeline_from_1500_transcripts/)
describes a radiation “delusion” and treats **“Skin Man”** as a nickname. The raw
source review does not establish either formulation. Independent `base.en` and
`small.en` ASR passes
materially agree that, around 01:49–01:55 of the complete 02:24 file, Daniel imagines
becoming a mutant and proposes _Skin Man_ as a superhero-style name. Nobody directly
heard the audio for this review, so this is an **unverified machine-audio lead**, not
a quotation or verified speech. It supports the term's likely source and hypothetical
mutant/superpower framing only—not a diagnosis, sincere radiation belief, or
recurring nickname.
Direct listening remains open in the maintainers' **Skin Man / radiation audio-review
queue**.

The archive transcript displays 01:49 as the whole record's duration even though the
mapped raw file runs about 02:24. That 35-second mismatch is why the locator follows
raw-media time rather than transcript time.

### 2017: a new Japan sequence and longer-stay framing

Two transcript records labelled September 22—_First day in Japan — I feel so
alone_ and _Japan adventure 2017 — Day 1_—resolve to media files with the same
SHA-1 hash. They should therefore be treated as alternate archive records for one
media object, not two independently corroborating videos.

This also corrects the supplied community timeline's **October 2017** relocation
marker. The preserved “Day 1” source trail begins on **September 22, 2017**. Separate
`base.en` and `small.en` passes over the raw opening materially agree in rendering
Daniel saying that he was in a room in Japan, had arrived roughly an hour earlier,
and had spoken with a manager about the room or contract. Nobody directly heard the
audio, so this is an unverified arrival account rather than an exact travel,
residence, city, or lease finding. The archive label itself is not proof that the
video was recorded or published on the day of arrival.

By December, the index contains _I’M STAYING IN JAPAN!_ and _Japanese apartment
tour — how much does rent cost_. Those titles document the channel's public framing
of a longer stay, while exact housing and immigration arrangements remain
unestablished by those titles.

### 2018: marriage-labelled material

The March 15 archive record is titled _WE GOT MARRIED_. This proves how the upload
was presented, not that a legal marriage occurred on or near the filename date.
Separate `base.en` and `small.en` passes materially agree at **02:06–02:44** that
Daniel said registration had happened several days earlier, called himself married,
acknowledged that he then had no paper to display, and expected documentation later.
At **05:51–06:14**, both render him describing the paperwork and signing as complete
and reporting that an official said they were technically married.

No reviewer directly heard the audio, no civil record was reviewed, and the generic
source does not identify the wife as Chihiro. Later application steps and witness,
family, and immigration detail are omitted. This is an unverified account attributed
to Daniel, not a legal-status or identity finding.

### 2020: a divorce-labelled upload

The February 18 archive record is titled _I am now Officially Divorced_. Its audio
has not been directly heard, and no civil record is cited on this page. Separate
`base.en` and `small.en` passes materially agree at **00:01–00:30** that Daniel said
he received an image presented to him as filed divorce paperwork. At
**01:29–02:44**, both render him attributing the breakdown to time spent on Discord
rather than with his wife, a lack of common ground and a language barrier, and an
inability or unwillingness to change. At **04:05–04:17**, he is machine-rendered
acknowledging uncertainty over whether the paper meant a finalized divorce or merely
that papers had been received.

The account remains Daniel's alone. No independently authenticated other-party
account was located in the bounded review, and the generic source does not identify
the wife as Chihiro. Cheating, court, and private-message details are excluded.

This title is a useful end marker for the core Japan/Chihiro publication sequence. It
does not establish a legal event, its date, or the other party's identity, and it does
not end all later Japan-related material.
