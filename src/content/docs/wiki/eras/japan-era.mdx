“The Japan era” is editorial shorthand for several Japan-centred runs of HIMR
material, not a claim that Daniel lived in Japan continuously. The archive documents
an early 2016 vlog sequence, another sequence beginning in September 2017, domestic
and relationship-labelled material during 2018–2019, and later Japan-labelled videos.
Dates on this page are
**archive record labels** unless a sentence explicitly says otherwise.

<Aside type="caution" title="Titles and first-person accounts">
  A title proves how an archived upload was labelled, not that every statement in it
  is true. Marriage, residence, and divorce wording below remains source-title
  language; no independent civil or immigration record has been reviewed.
</Aside>

## Chronology

### 2016: the first archived Japan vlog run

One clearly Japan-labelled sequence in the preserved 2016 index includes
*Thai festival Yoyogi park — Japan Vlog day 2* on May 15. The linked raw video is
present in Internet Archive item `69999`. A July 29 record is titled *leaving japan
forever,* but that label is not treated as proof of a permanent move. Together, the
titles establish a Japan-centred run in the archive; they do not establish exact
arrival, residency, or departure dates.

### 2017: a new Japan sequence and longer-stay framing

Two transcript records labelled September 22—*First day in Japan — I feel so
alone* and *Japan adventure 2017 — Day 1*—resolve to media files with the same
SHA-1 hash. They should therefore be treated as alternate archive records for one
media object, not two independently corroborating videos.

By December, the index contains *I’M STAYING IN JAPAN!* and *Japanese apartment
tour — how much does rent cost*. Those titles document the channel's public framing
of a longer stay, while exact housing and immigration arrangements remain
unestablished by those titles.

### 2018: marriage-labelled material

The March 15 archive record is titled *WE GOT MARRIED*. This proves how the upload
was presented, not that a legal marriage occurred on or near the filename date. The
unchecked machine transcript offers a possible announcement locator at 02:06–02:18,
but that locator is retained for future audio review rather than used as public
evidence.

Later 2018 records include joint and domestic labels such as *Mcdonalds Q&A with my
Wife*. Separately, 2019 titles explicitly name Chihiro. Connecting every generic
“wife” title to a specific legal identity would require a stronger audio and visual
review than this page has completed.

### 2020: a divorce-labelled upload

The February 18 archive record is titled *I am now Officially Divorced*. Its audio
has not been directly reviewed, and no civil record is cited on this page.
This page therefore establishes only that the archive contains a divorce-labelled
update; it does not infer a legal event or date from the title.

This title is a useful end marker for the core Japan/Chihiro publication sequence. It
does not establish the date of a legal event or end all later Japan-related material.

### Later Japan-labelled records

Japan remained a recurring subject in archive titles. For example, the preserved
archive links raw video to a record labelled *Exploring the Slums of Osaka in Japan*
on March 30, 2023. It establishes a later Japan-labelled record, not the truth of the
title's characterization.
