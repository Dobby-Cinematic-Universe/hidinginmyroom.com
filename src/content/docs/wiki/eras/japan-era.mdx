“The Japan era” is editorial shorthand for several Japan-centred runs of HIMR
material, not a claim that Daniel lived in Japan continuously. The archive documents
Daniel's retrospective 2010–2014 travel accounts, an early 2016 vlog sequence,
another sequence beginning in September 2017, domestic and relationship-labelled
material during 2018–2019, and later Japan-labelled videos.
Dates on this page are
**archive record labels** unless a sentence explicitly says otherwise.

<Aside type="caution" title="Titles and first-person accounts">
  A title proves how an archived upload was labelled, not that every statement
  in it is true. Marriage, residence, and divorce wording below remains
  source-title language; no independent civil or immigration record has been
  reviewed.
</Aside>

## Chronology

### Retrospective 2010–2014 Japan and Korea accounts

Five Patreon records published in 2017 look back further than the preserved 2016
video run. The letters below use the [Patreon Source Catalogue's access
scheme](/wiki/archive/patreon-source-catalog/#access-boundary): **P** means the full
body was visible signed out at the dated audit, while **L** means the body-derived
summary comes from the access-controlled preservation copy. No Patreon image or
locked prose is reproduced here.

The separate [Patreon partner and social-role
map](/wiki/events/relationship-dramas/#patreon-retrospectives-earlier-partner-and-social-roles)
reconciles the 2011 guesthouse/2014 near-marriage cluster, first in-person Japanese
girlfriend, online first girlfriend, Korean and French former-girlfriend, and
Taiwanese social labels without treating overlapping posts as identity proof.

Daniel claims in the locked body of [_Studying
Korean_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/studying-korean-10323546)
(May 4, 2017, **L**) that its photograph was taken while he was in Osaka in 2010,
where he remembers studying Korean each day. In another locked retrospective,
[_I used to have a social
life_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/i-used-to-have-11616138)
(June 7, **L**), Daniel characterizes Japan as both an escape from England and a
place where he once had a more active social life. The latter is a subjective account
of why he returned, not travel documentation or evidence about anyone pictured.

Two South-Korea-labelled posts preserve separate year markers. Daniel claims in
[_Me and my cousins in South
Korea_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/me-and-my-in-12021814)
(June 24, **L**) that Malaysian relatives joined him in South Korea in 2014 and that
he navigated into Seoul independently on that occasion. The July 19 public title
[_Me in Korea
2012_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/me-in-korea-2012-13132089)
(**L**) supplies a separate creator-used 2012 label. These records can describe
different visits; the archive does not collapse them into one trip, authenticate the
photographs' dates, or treat Daniel's recollection as his relatives' or former
girlfriend's account.

The strongest reader-accessible detail is the public body of [_Room I stayed in
during first trip to
Japan_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/room-i-stayed-in-12990510)
(July 15, **P**). Daniel says he used the pictured Osaka room for five months on his
first Japan trip, about seven years before the post, and associates that trip with a
laptop his father bought for him. That supports a **five-month recollection**, not a
verified 2010 lease, continuous residence, address, immigration category, or exact
arrival and departure dates. His references in the same public post to later returns
also remain retrospective self-report.

### July 2016: a radiation-themed creator title

The same run contains a July 1 record titled _Japan's radiation problem — They lied
to us_. This establishes that Daniel published a radiation-themed first-person
commentary, not that he was exposed to radiation, had a particular medical condition,
or that an unnamed party lied.

describes a radiation “delusion” and treats **“Skin Man”** as a nickname. The raw
source review does not establish either formulation. Two independent transcription passes
materially agree that, around 01:49–01:55 of the complete 02:24 file, Daniel imagines
becoming a mutant and proposes _Skin Man_ as a superhero-style name. Nobody directly
heard the audio for this review, so this is an **unverified machine-audio lead**, not
a quotation or verified speech. It supports the term's likely source and hypothetical
mutant/superpower framing only—not a diagnosis, sincere radiation belief, or
recurring nickname.
Direct listening remains open in the maintainers' **Skin Man / radiation audio-review
queue**.

The archive transcript displays 01:49 as the whole record's duration even though the
mapped raw file runs about 02:24. That 35-second mismatch is why the locator follows
raw-media time rather than transcript time.
