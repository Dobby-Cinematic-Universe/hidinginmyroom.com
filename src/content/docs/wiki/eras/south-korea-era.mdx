The **South Korea era** is editorial shorthand for an early-2020 run of Korea-labelled HIMR videos. It combines a proposed move, urban outings and everyday room-and-cooking content. It is not a claim about continuous residence, immigration status or the exact beginning and end of a trip. Earlier Korea recollections belong to the [Japan-era retrospective chronology](/wiki/eras/japan-era/#retrospective-20102014-japan-and-korea-accounts).

## Outings and everyday content

The Seoul-labelled outing supplies a different format from the announcement. Sampled raw frames show an urban square at **03:10**, a night skyline at **05:45**, and food being handled over a tabletop grill at **07:50**. These are observations about the preserved footage, not confirmation of every place name or the accompanying conversation. Guest-related research is kept in the [friends directory](/wiki/characters/friends-and-guests/), separate from the era's travel sequence.

## Scope and open questions

This page separates the visible video formats from machine-rendered speech and archive labels. It does not establish an arrival or departure date, independently verify rent, adopt relationship or visa assertions from the announcement, or merge the people in different outings. Source links are provided instead of copying room or street images where a reuse basis has not been established.
