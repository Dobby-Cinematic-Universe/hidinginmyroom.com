The **South Korea era** brings together separate Korea-labelled runs of HIMR videos:
everyday outings in **2016**, followed by a proposed move, urban outings and
room-and-cooking content in **early 2020**. It is not a claim about continuous
residence, immigration status or the exact beginning and end of either trip.
Earlier Korea recollections belong to the [Japan-era retrospective
chronology](/wiki/eras/japan-era/#retrospective-20102014-japan-and-korea-accounts),
while the [language-learning videos](/wiki/eras/japan-era/#language-learning-videos-20172023)
include a separate Korean-study account.
