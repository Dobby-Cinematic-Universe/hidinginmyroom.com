“UK Delivery & Travel Era” is a navigation label for two evidence layers: earlier
moving and travel records with archived video, and a late-2024 cluster whose titles
refer to Amazon delivery work. It is **not** evidence of one continuous job or a
complete employment history.

<Aside type="caution" title="The archive changes at 2024">
  All 2021 and 2022 transcript records in the dated snapshot have linked archived
  video. None of the 99 records labeled 2024 or the 49 records labeled 2025 has a
  raw-video URL. Accordingly, the later delivery section reports source titles and
  coverage gaps—not events inferred from unchecked machine transcripts.
</Aside>

### What the earlier archive does not establish

No 2021 title in the primary index contains *Deliveroo*, *Just Eat*, *Uber Eats*,
*courier*, or *food delivery*. That does not prove Daniel did no such work; it means
the old NotebookLM claim about a 2021 courier career cannot yet be published from
title metadata. A raw segment with a locator, or a suitable independent record, is
still needed.

## 2025 boundary

The 2025 index contains 49 transcript records and no linked raw video. Its titles do
not directly name Amazon Flex, a courier service, or a delivery role. Generic labels
such as *started my new job today* cannot safely be assigned to delivery work from
the title alone. Machine summaries may help locate future research, but they are not
used as evidence here.

## What would raise confidence

- Recover the original 2024–2025 videos and reconcile them to the transcript IDs.
- Check each relevant segment against raw audio/video with timestamps.
- Seek a first-party platform notice or other direct record before describing any
  account action as fact.
- Keep travel, vehicle use, and employment status separate unless a source explicitly
  connects them.
