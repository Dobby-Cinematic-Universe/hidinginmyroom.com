“UK Delivery & Travel Era” is a navigation label for two evidence layers: earlier
moving and travel records with archived video, and a late-2024 cluster whose titles
refer to Amazon delivery work. It is **not** evidence of one continuous job or a
complete employment history.

<Aside type="caution" title="The archive changes at 2024">
  All 2021 and 2022 transcript records in the August 25 dated snapshot had
  linked archived video. None of its 99 rows labelled 2024 or 49 rows labelled
  2025 carried a raw-video URL. Supplemental inventories reconciled on August 27
  now map the late-2024 files cited below and other individual later records.
  Accordingly, the later delivery section reports source titles and review
  gaps—not events inferred from unchecked machine transcripts.
</Aside>

### What the earlier archive does not establish

No 2021 title in the primary index contains _Deliveroo_, _Just Eat_, _Uber Eats_,
_courier_, or _food delivery_. That does not prove Daniel did no such work; it means
the old NotebookLM claim about a 2021 courier career cannot yet be published from
title metadata. A raw segment with a locator, or a suitable independent record, is
still needed.

## 2025 boundary

The August 25 legacy index contains 49 transcript records and carried no linked raw
video for them. Supplemental reconciliation has since located raw files for some
individual 2025 rows, but its titles do not directly name Amazon Flex, a courier
service, or a delivery role. Generic labels such as _started my new job today_ cannot
safely be assigned to delivery work from the title alone. Machine summaries may help
locate future research, but they are not used as evidence here.

## What would raise confidence

- Complete reconciliation and integrity checks for the remaining 2024–2025 records.
- Check each relevant segment against raw audio/video with timestamps.
- Seek a first-party platform notice or other direct record before describing any
  account action as fact.
- Keep travel, vehicle use, and employment status separate unless a source explicitly
  connects them.
