“The Thailand era” is editorial shorthand for a Thailand-centred run of HIMR
records, not a verified period of continuous residence. The preserved title sequence
has a Malaysia-labelled prelude in mid-2022, a Thailand-move-labelled transition in
October 2022, Vietnam-trip-labelled records in January 2023, and further
Thailand-labelled records through early 2024. Dates below are **archive record
labels** unless stated otherwise.

<Aside type="caution" title="What the sources can prove">
  A title such as “moving,” “back,” or “living after one year” establishes the
  creator's public framing. It does not independently prove an address, immigration
  status, exact travel date, or uninterrupted stay. This page omits private-location
  details and does not repeat allegations about people around Daniel.
</Aside>

## 2022: regional prelude and the Thailand-labelled transition

### Malaysia in June and July

The source-title sequence begins before the Thailand-move-labelled run. The archive labels a June 30
video *Why I’m moving to Malaysia*, followed by Malaysia-labelled records from July
2 through July 9 and *Why i had to Leave Malaysia* on July 13. The June 30 and July
13 boundary records both resolve to raw media in Internet Archive item `699992` and
bracket a Malaysia-focused title run; “moving” remains the title's wording rather
than an independently verified relocation.

### Thailand-move-labelled sequence

Three source titles form a chronological label sequence:

- September 21: *I M MOVING TO THAILAND (soon)*.
- October 5: *Last Stream in the UK before i go to THAILAND*.
- October 10: *Moving to Thailand to meet my Thai girlfriend!*.

The October 10 raw video was manually spot-checked at 07:45, 08:20, 10:55,
and 12:00. Those frames visibly show an elevator/corridor sequence, suite signage,
and the interior of a multi-room hotel suite. The checked footage verifies the
hotel sequence, while the destination and relationship context depend on the archive
title.

## 2023: Vietnam- and Thailand-labelled catalogue expansion

### Vietnam-trip-labelled records

The January index contains a Vietnam-labelled run between Thailand-labelled records. *We
went to Vietnam* and *Eating $11 curry Steak in Vietnam*, both labelled January 6,
share one raw-video URL and SHA-1 hash. Likewise, the January 10 titles *$12
overnight sleeper bus in Vietnam* and *DO NOT ride the sleeper bus in Vietnam* share
one media object. Counting each title as an independent source would therefore
overstate the evidence.

### Return-labelled and Phuket material

The archive labels a May 9 record *I’m BACK in Thailand*.
On May 29 it records *Honeymoon trip with my Girlfriend in Phuket — 1 year together
❤️*. That media URL is also attached to a May 30 transcript record titled *My Trip
to Phuket — Amazing Islands*, so those two records are not independent sources.

The July 17 record *what its really like living in Thailand after 1 year* is a useful
creator-defined milestone. It is not an exact anniversary: the archive's October
2022 meeting-labelled sequence and the title's “after 1 year” wording may be measuring
different things.

### August 2023 Ice Poseidon sequence

The archive preserves an August 6 raw video titled [*Confronting Ice Poseidon in
real life*](https://archive.org/download/699992/20230806-Confronting%20Ice%20Poseidon%20in%20real%20life-4SFcyqjsRUg.mp4).
Direct frame checks across the complete 09:53.85 file show Daniel filming in a public
shopping-centre setting and an on-camera encounter near the end; the source title
supplies the Ice Poseidon label. The frames do not establish what was said or any
later legal event.

More importantly, the immediate archive chronology does not support the community
timeline's claim that Daniel promptly fled Thailand to avoid association after an
August incident. It preserves an [August 7 apology-labelled
video](https://archive.org/download/699992/20230807-my%20apology%20to%20Deepak%20and%20Ice%20Poseidon-7823b5a0-76cf-47fd-a353-3c4b378ecb4a.mp4),
an [August 10 Thailand cat-café-labelled
video](https://archive.org/download/699992/20230810-Visiting%20the%20best%20Cat%20Cafe%20in%20Thailand%20with%20my%20Girlfriend-o00TuDfJ_ns.mp4),
and Ice-stuck-in-Thailand commentary labelled [August
12](https://archive.org/download/699992/20230812-updates%20on%20ice%20poseidon%20being%20stuck%20in%20thailand%20-%20my%20thoughts-3dfe37d9-f649-4e43-9037-bda8828e2c67.mp4)
and [August
18](https://archive.org/download/699992/20230818-updates%20on%20ice%20poseidon%20being%20stuck%20in%20thailand%20-%20my%20thoughts-ddc6f1aa-2a14-4d6e-bd69-e76906f4cd5c.mp4).
Other Thailand-labelled records also continue between those dates. That sequence
cannot prove Daniel's physical location on every day. It does not support the
proposed **immediate-flight** chronology or motive.

## 2024 and the end boundary

The transcript index contains Thailand-labelled titles in early 2024, including
*last trip with my girlfriend before i leave Thailand FOREVER*
(January 12), *my Last month in Thailand* (February 2), and *room tour of my Luxury
Thailand Apartment* (March 8). None has a raw-video URL in the August 25, 2026
manifest. They are valid archive records but weaker evidence than the 2022–2023
video-matched sequence.

The snapshot therefore does **not** establish one exact departure date. For the
related generic relationship labels, see
[Thai-girlfriend-labelled records](/wiki/characters/thai-girlfriend/).
