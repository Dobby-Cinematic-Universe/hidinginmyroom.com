“The Thailand era” is editorial shorthand for a Thailand-centred run of HIMR
records, not a verified period of continuous residence. The preserved title sequence
has an earlier Malaysia-trip account in 2018, a separate Malaysia-labelled run in
mid-2022, a Thailand-move-labelled transition in October 2022, Vietnam-trip-labelled
records in January 2023, and further Thailand-labelled records through early 2024.
Dates below are **archive record labels** unless stated otherwise.

<Aside type="caution" title="What the sources can prove">
  A title such as “moving,” “back,” or “living after one year” establishes the
  creator's public framing. It does not independently prove an address,
  immigration status, exact travel date, or uninterrupted stay. This page omits
  private-location details and does not repeat allegations about people around
  Daniel.
</Aside>

## 2018: Patreon Malaysia-trip prelude

Three access-controlled Patreon bodies add a planning-to-return sequence four years
before the public 2022 Malaysia-labelled videos. All three are **L** under the
[Patreon Source Catalogue's access
scheme](/wiki/archive/patreon-source-catalog/#access-boundary): their public pages
expose title/date metadata, while the summaries below come from the retained locked
bodies. They are Daniel's accounts, not passenger, border, lodging, or family records;
no attachment, private itinerary, or exact location is reproduced.

- In [_The Malaysia
  trip_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/malaysia-trip-18212709)
  (April 16, 2018), Daniel claims he, his wife, mother, and sister were discussing a
  family trip and that he was comparing travel and lodging options. He describes the
  plan as dependent on other people's availability. This establishes planning in his
  account, not a booking or completed journey.
- In [_Malaysia
  preparations_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/malaysia-18311158)
  (April 21), Daniel claims he was weighing camera, luggage, clothing, and other
  practical choices for a roughly two-week trip expected to include islands. His
  comments about possibly remaining in Japan are expectations, not evidence of a
  residence decision or immigration outcome.
- In [_Island
  life_](https://www.patreon.com/Hidinginmyroom/posts/island-life-19195043)
  (June 2), Daniel claims he was then on an island with the family group, describes
  swimming and beach activity, and says he expected to return to Japan the following
  week and resume his usual videos. The body does not name the island; its placement
  in the Malaysia planning sequence is not independent location confirmation.

<Aside type="caution" title="One creator's travel sequence, not three independent confirmations">
  The planning, preparation, and on-trip posts are all written by Daniel. Their
  chronological fit makes them useful as a sequence, but repetition cannot verify
  the destination, participants, itinerary, duration, residence, or return.
</Aside>

## 2022: regional prelude and the Thailand-labelled transition

### Malaysia in June and July

The source-title sequence begins before the Thailand-move-labelled run. The archive labels a June 30
video _Why I’m moving to Malaysia_, followed by Malaysia-labelled records from July
2 through July 9 and _Why i had to Leave Malaysia_ on July 13. The June 30 and July
13 boundary records both resolve to raw media in Internet Archive item `699992` and
bracket a Malaysia-focused title run; “moving” remains the title's wording rather
than an independently verified relocation.

### Thailand-move-labelled sequence

Three source titles form a chronological label sequence:

- September 21: _I M MOVING TO THAILAND (soon)_.
- October 5: _Last Stream in the UK before i go to THAILAND_.
- October 10: _Moving to Thailand to meet my Thai girlfriend!_.

The October 10 raw video was manually spot-checked at 07:45, 08:20, 10:55,
and 12:00. Those frames visibly show an elevator/corridor sequence, suite signage,
and the interior of a multi-room hotel suite. The checked footage verifies the
hotel sequence, while the destination and relationship context depend on the archive
title.

## 2024 and the end boundary

The transcript index contains Thailand-labelled titles in early 2024, including
_last trip with my girlfriend before i leave Thailand FOREVER_
(January 12), _my Last month in Thailand_ (February 2), and _room tour of my Luxury
Thailand Apartment_ (March 8). None had a raw-video URL in the August 25, 2026
manifest; supplemental archive reconciliation on August 27 located candidate raw
files for these cited records. Because their contents have not been reviewed, they
remain weaker evidence than the 2022–2023 video-checked sequence.

The snapshot therefore does **not** establish one exact departure date. For the
related generic relationship labels, see
[Thai-girlfriend-labelled records](/wiki/characters/thai-girlfriend/).

### February 2024: Thai-language study retrospective

The February 11, 2024 filename-labelled _my Experience Living in Thailand on a
Student Visa and Learning Thai_ adds a study account to the travel chronology.
Its machine transcript renders Daniel saying that he had attended a Thai-language
course for several months and completed it. Later, it renders him saying that the
classes had finished a couple of months earlier and that he was spending the
remainder of his claimed student-visa period in Thailand. This is his account,
not an independently established enrolment, qualification, immigration status or
departure date. The closing shorthand about nearly a year at language school
should not be converted into a year of classroom attendance: the earlier passage
distinguishes the classes from the longer period he describes staying there.

### An undated, mixed retrospective

The recovered [_why i was unhappy living in
Thailand_](https://archive.org/download/hidinginmyroom2/why%20i%20was%20unhappy%20living%20in%20Thailand%20%5BAs-KGkkAH5M%5D.mp4)
does not simply end with a rejection of the country. The machine transcript
renders Daniel criticizing the traffic and difficulty of walking around Bangkok,
and describing Thai food as poorly matched to his preferences. In its closing
passage, however, it renders him saying he would like to return eventually,
valuing affordability and his experience of the city's social atmosphere, and
preferring Thailand to Japan if choosing where to live. These are his personal
evaluations, not factual assessments of either country's people or living
conditions. The source is **undated** in the retained catalogue; its opening
reference to recently returning to the UK cannot establish when it was recorded.
