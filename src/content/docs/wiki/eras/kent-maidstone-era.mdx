## Archive coverage

The [2015 source reconciliation](/wiki/archive/2015-source-catalog/) identifies **32
canonical media identities**. Twenty-six are linked from transcript records; six are
present in the raw-media metadata without a corresponding transcript record.

The earliest three located filename dates precede the transcript archive's October
starting point:

| Filename date | Archive label                               | Transcript coverage |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| 2015-07-06    | _Lonely guy animation_                      | Raw media only      |
| 2015-08-06    | _How to draw backgrounds for manga, comics_ | Raw media only      |
| 2015-09-13    | _Cat purring sound effect ASMR_             | Raw media only      |

<Aside type="caution" title="What these dates mean">
  These are dates embedded in Internet Archive filenames. They are useful for
  sorting the collection but are not yet independently verified original-upload
  dates.
</Aside>

## Formats labelled in the 2015 record

### Travel- and outing-labelled records

The October archive labels include a [Wing Yip supermarket
vlog](https://archive.org/download/69999/20151001-Going%20to%20wing%20yip%20asian%20supermarket%20groceries%20buying%20and%20crabs%21%20VLOG%20with%20daniel%21-wYvH9H9eLeQ.mp4),
a [Heathrow pod ride](https://archive.org/download/69999/20151005-riding%20the%20Heathrow%20pod%20network%20the%20future%20is%20here%20%21-woEdz1DmhPg.mp4),
a [Stavanger walking video](https://archive.org/download/69999/20151005-walking%20around%20stavanger%20in%20norway%21%20travel%20VLOG-wGJ-cK8eowQ.mp4),
and a [bus journey toward Bergen](https://archive.org/download/69999/20151007-riding%20the%20bus%20from%20stavanger%20to%20bergen%20in%20Norway-sk9LLSU8ufQ.mp4).
Those labels show that travel and ordinary outings were part of the preserved record
alongside room-based videos.

### Xcode- and game-development-labelled records

At least thirteen located media identities from October through December are labeled
as Xcode tutorials, iOS projects, or game-development updates. The sequence includes
a Flappy Bird-style exercise, a Space Invaders-style project, zombie-game revisions,
button and sound tutorials, and platformer work. This is a prominent repeated topic
in the located 2015 titles.

Representative primary records include:

- [I Made a game!!! creating flappy bird game on Xcode 7](https://archive.org/download/69999/20151011-I%20Made%20a%20game%21%21%21%20creating%20flappy%20bird%20game%20on%20xcode%207%20on%20macbook%20air-F8_oH2eu70o.mp4)

## What remains unverified

The old NotebookLM dossier adds biographical interpretation about residence,
employment, benefits, family life, and motives. Those claims are not repeated here
because the current review has established the media inventory and selected visible
content—not the complete off-camera history. They can be added later only with
attributed, claim-level evidence.
