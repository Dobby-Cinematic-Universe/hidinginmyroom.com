## Epilogue: the 2022 HIMR game project

A brief later coda connects those 2015 game-development labels to a public Patreon
project; it does not extend the geographic era itself into 2022. On **January 2,
2022**, Daniel wrote that he had returned to game development, moved from trying to
relearn Xcode to using **GameMaker Studio 2** and a tutorial-derived foundation, and
planned a game in which the player would control him through levels, references, and
characters recognizable from his channel. These are Daniel's descriptions of his
process and plan, not independent verification of when development began or how much
original work had been completed.

### What Daniel's locked patch notes describe

The retained bodies add a version-by-version feature map. These are **Daniel's patch
notes**, not findings from executing or testing the builds:

- **Version 1.0:** keyboard and mouse controls for opening the computer/mail screen,
  shooting, limited slow motion, grenades, purchasing a grenade, and jumping. Daniel
  also described a small in-game payment for defeating an enemy.
- **Version 1.1:** an eight-heart health display, a companion character who could heal
  and follow the player, randomized enemy size and speed, doors, spikes, slow-motion
  pickups, two weapon types, and several acknowledged collision/spawn bugs. His future
  plan mentioned spending in-game money through the computer, eventually buying a
  ticket to a Japan level, adding a petrol-station night-shift minigame, and collecting
  tapes tied to older channel material.
- **Version 1.6:** save/load keys; quests, quest givers, and dialogue; a computer menu
  for ammunition, a planned Japan ticket, simulated YouTube uploads, randomly moving
  “stocks,” and email rewards; sleeping to restore health; a petrol-station scanning
  minigame; a companion and “Baby Daniels”; a death/respawn money penalty; limited
  ammunition; and a reset key. Daniel listed more quests, money systems, dream levels,
  and house upgrades as future work. He also attributed slower progress to moving to
  Scotland, which remains a creator-written travel/move claim rather than a residence
  record.

### August 2026 Discord retrospective

Six adjacent August 29 messages add a much later creator-side callback. Daniel's
self-identifying Discord account said the project was on Patreon, recalled putting
substantial effort into it, believed it should still work, said he no longer
remembered how to code, and contrasted the older work with what generative AI might
make easier now. These are present-day recollections and opinions, not technical
evidence about the retained build.

The account casually labelled the work **“2021 pre AI times.”** The reconciled public
Patreon records above were actually published from **January 2 to January 24, 2022**.
The wiki therefore preserves the Discord wording as an imprecise retrospective rather
than moving the dated release sequence into 2021. The AI remark also does not show
that Daniel used AI on the original project or created a later AI-assisted version.
