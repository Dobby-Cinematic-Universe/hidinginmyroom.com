The wiki is organized around the surviving source record, not an attempt to turn
every upload title or community nickname into biography. Coverage is intentionally
uneven: a **source-matched** page may establish only that a record exists, while a
**video-checked** claim identifies the exact visual or audiovisual scope that was
reviewed.

Frozen legacy-index snapshot: **August 25, 2026** · **2,227 transcript records** ·
**2,043 rows carried archived raw-video links** · **184 did not**. Supplemental
Internet Archive reconciliation on August 27 recovered additional raw files without
rewriting those historical counts or upgrading any content-review status.
Current-platform observations are kept in a separate dated layer.
