an evidence limitation: the transcript can suggest where to research, but cannot by
itself support a “video checked” claim.

## How to use this timeline

- Follow an era or person link for reviewed narrative context.
- Treat a title as the archive's label, not proof that the title's claim is true.
- Prefer claims with a checked citation, then read the citation's stated visual or
  audiovisual scope; a page can remain **Source matched** when only selected segments
  have been checked.
- Consult the [verification method](/wiki/archive/methodology/) for date, duplicate,
  and transcript cautions.
