**Daniel Lord** is the public name used for the creator documented by this wiki. His
current YouTube channel is displayed as **Hiding in my room** at
[`@hidinginmyroom1111`](https://www.youtube.com/@hidinginmyroom1111). The exact
capitalization has varied in archive and community labels; this wiki abbreviates the
project as **HIMR**.

“Daniel” appears in multiple public source labels, including the current
channel's [*Cooking with Daniel — Garden Ritual*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DW2nBIWbey4).
The full form “Daniel Lord” appears in an archived 2019 video title. Those public
labels justify the article name; they are not presented as proof of a legal name or
as permission to compile private identifying information.

## What this page does not claim

<Aside type="caution" title="Public work, not total biography">
  A large first-person archive is still not an independently verified life record.
  This overview does not publish an exact birth date, home address, legal identity,
  private employment file, medical diagnosis, or off-camera relationship history.
</Aside>

- A title such as “I moved,” “I married,” or “I bought” establishes a source label,
  not the legal event, ownership, date, or other person's account.
- A checked video can establish what Daniel said and what the recording shows. His
  description of an off-camera event remains a creator statement unless corroborated.
- Community posts remain secondary sources. A title can establish only its own
  wording; a fully reviewed public repost can support a narrow observation about what
  that copy appears to show or say. Neither proves an off-camera event or allegation.
- The [research dossiers](/wiki/archive/research-dossiers/) produced from the removed
  NotebookLM notebooks remain quarantined drafts, not citations.
