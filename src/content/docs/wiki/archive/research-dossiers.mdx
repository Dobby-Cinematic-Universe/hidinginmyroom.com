The maintainers' private research workspace contains NotebookLM-assisted dossiers for
2015–2025 and an all-time overview. They are retained as **research indexes** because
their chronology, counts, entity links, and summaries can help locate relevant source
material. They are not included in this public repository.

<Aside type="danger" title="Do not cite these drafts">
  The dossiers were generated from machine transcripts. A polished summary can repeat
  recognition errors, conflate records, overstate a creator's account, or inherit bad
  timestamps. Copying a dossier paragraph into an article does not count as review.
</Aside>

## Permitted use

The drafts may be used to:

- find likely titles and transcript records;
- generate search terms;
- identify a possible sequence of videos; and
- propose atomic claims for the claim ledger.

They may not be used to establish that an event happened, a date is authoritative, a
quotation is accurate, or two names refer to the same person.

## Promotion path

1. Locate the candidate record in the dated source manifest.
2. Resolve duplicates, missing media, and date-basis warnings.
3. Open the raw video or other underlying source.
4. Record the exact evidence and its scope in the claim ledger.
5. Apply the [verification method](/wiki/archive/methodology/).
6. Publish only the wording supported at the resulting verification level.

The first source-identity reconciliation is available in the
[2015 Source Catalogue](/wiki/archive/2015-source-catalog/). It deliberately contains
no NotebookLM summaries.
