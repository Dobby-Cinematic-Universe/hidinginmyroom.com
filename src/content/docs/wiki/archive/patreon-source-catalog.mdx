This catalogue records publication-safe **metadata and narrowly attributed source
readings** for the acquired _Hidinginmyroom — Daniel's Patreon_ preservation set. It
publishes aggregate counts, public post locators, and limited claim summaries; it does
not publish the downloaded corpus or reproduce locked post bodies.

For the corpus-wide findings—the 2016–2022 editorial phases, edit-state caveat,
creator-strategy pattern, corrections, and campaign-object history—see the
[Patreon Longitudinal Analysis](/wiki/archive/patreon-longitudinal-analysis/). This
page remains the post-level locator and access-state catalogue.

<Aside type="danger" title="Private archive boundary">
  Post bodies, downloaded assets, raw API JSON, status caches, and raw or signed
  media URLs remain private. They must not be linked, copied, or treated as a
  public mirror. Patreon access fields—including paid, viewable, pledge, and
  entitlement flags—describe a particular record or session. **They are not proof
  that a post was public, free, or lawfully redistributable.** An authenticated
  download is not evidence of public access either.
</Aside>
