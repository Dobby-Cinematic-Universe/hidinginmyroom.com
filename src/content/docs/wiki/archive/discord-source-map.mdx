independent chronology. After the identity section, “Daniel said” is shorthand for
“the exported account, which later identified itself as Daniel, said.”

  Message permalinks and bounded paraphrases remain the default. One independently
  reviewed, Pushkin-captioned cat image is reproduced on the pets page under the
  contributor's public-source representation; its metadata was stripped and no
  signed attachment URL is exposed. No other supplied image, audio, video, profile
  identifier, plate, home, live location, employer clue, private-party identifier,
  file hash, or media metadata is reproduced. An attachment beside a self-identifying
  message is merely account-presented media, not biometric evidence. Allegations and
  boasts below establish only that the account made the claim.

  publication-worthy HIMR event cluster outside the routes above. Unrouted material

  unrelated film, game, music, celebrity, religion, politics, and general-opinion

  greetings and contextless reactions; graphic sexual jokes; and private
  first-name-only or transient-person references. Those categories are omitted for
  irrelevance, missing context, or privacy—not silently converted into biography.

  the pets page under the contributor's public-source representation. The other 330
  remain unembedded. Public message permalinks alone do not grant a licence to mirror
  image, audio, or video payloads.
- Machine-rendered voice remains unverified even where the two offline passes agree
  and a selected transcription is published. **Machine-generated and unreviewed;
  may be wrong; not a verified quotation.** No voiceprint, face match, or other
  biometric conclusion is made.
- Private locations, travel routes, homes, workplaces, plates, contacts, family
  health and finances, and third-party intimate detail are omitted even when an
  exported message gestures toward them.
- One 28 July discussion describes alleged sexual conduct by an unnamed former
  partner and disputes how it should be labelled. It is not linked or summarized
  further because the export lacks that person’s account and publication would
  expose third-party intimate detail; this omission is not a finding about consent

  username statement. Those account links need separate public-source evidence;

- A Discord claim may be used to show that the account made a claim on a given date.
