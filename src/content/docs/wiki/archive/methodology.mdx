The HIMR Wiki uses transcripts to **find** evidence, not as evidence by default. A
published claim must say no more than its cited source can establish.

<Aside type="caution" title="A primary video is not automatic proof">
  A raw video can establish what a person said or what the recording visibly shows.
  If Daniel describes an off-camera event, the safe formulation is “Daniel said…”
  unless an independent source corroborates the event.
</Aside>

## Current archive audit

On August 25, 2026, the project preserved the live
[transcript index](https://himr-archive.pages.dev/data/index.js) and metadata for
[Internet Archive item 69999](https://archive.org/details/69999) and
[item 699992](https://archive.org/details/699992). The automated reconciliation found:

| Finding | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Transcript records | 2,227 |
| Records with an Internet Archive video URL | 2,043 |
| Records without a video URL | 184 |
| Distinct linked video URLs | 1,919 |
| Shared video-URL groups | 67 |
| Records participating in those shared groups | 191 |
| Records over 250 displayed words per minute | 55 |
| Records with a malformed displayed duration | 4 |

These are **record counts**, not a verified count of distinct channel videos. A shared
URL may be a duplicate, a chunk of a longer stream, or a mapping error. An implausible
speech rate usually indicates suspect duration or timestamp metadata and triggers
manual review.

The dated source responses, hashes, normalized manifest, and audit are retained in
the maintainers' private evidence store. Future snapshots do not silently replace
earlier ones.

## Evidence ladder

| Material | How the wiki uses it |
| --- | --- |
| Raw video or audio | Verify exact speech and visible action at a stated time |
| Human-corrected segment | Quote or closely paraphrase only the checked passage |
| Machine transcript | Locate a likely passage; never treat it as evidence, fact, or a quotation |
| Title or filename | Describe the archive label, not the truth of the title or date |
| First-party statement | Attribute the statement to its author |
| Independent reliable record | Corroborate only the facts within that record's scope |
| Reviewed public community image or repost | Establish only what the complete preserved copy appears to show or say, with provenance limits |
| Other community post or wiki | Establish community usage, record an attributed allegation, or generate a research lead |
| AI or NotebookLM output | Discover topics; never cite as evidence |

Automatic captions and locally generated speech recognition are machine transcripts,
even when they were produced from the original media file. They can supply a candidate
timestamp. A spoken quotation or close paraphrase becomes **video checked** only when
a human reviewer directly listens to the raw segment; reviewing frames does not count
as listening to the soundtrack.

<Aside type="caution" title="Narrow public research-lead exception">
  Benign machine-only material may appear publicly only as a conspicuously labelled
  **unverified research lead**. The entry must provide the exact source, candidate
  locator, whether raw media is mapped, available, or missing, and the named
  human-review queue. It is never evidence, a fact, a quotation, or verified speech.
  Machine-only serious wrongdoing, sexualized claims involving minors, and invasive
  private, relationship, financial, travel, or medical detail remain outside the
  public article until the relevant verification and sensitivity safeguards are met.
</Aside>

## Verification states

- <VerificationBadge status="unverified" /> — imported or proposed; research only,
  except for the narrowly labelled benign research-navigation exception above.
- <VerificationBadge status="source-matched" /> — the source identity exists and has
  been reconciled; its contents are still unchecked.
- <VerificationBadge status="video-checked" /> — the cited raw-media segment and
  surrounding context were manually reviewed.
- <VerificationBadge status="community-media-checked" /> — the complete available
  repost and post context were reviewed; no obvious substantive edit or credible
  authenticity dispute was found, but the original is not authenticated.
- <VerificationBadge status="corroborated" /> — an appropriate independent source
  also supports the claim.
- <VerificationBadge status="disputed" /> — credible sources conflict and the wiki
  preserves the disagreement.

## From search result to published sentence

<Steps>
1. **Draft one atomic claim.** Avoid combining several facts under one citation.
2. **Locate candidate evidence.** Search the transcript archive and source manifest.
3. **Open the underlying source.** Review the raw segment with surrounding context.
4. **If only a repost survives, review the whole copy.** Preserve its direct media
   URL, type, bytes, hash, post context, edit assessment, and any authenticity dispute.
5. **Record the evidence scope.** Mark self-report, visible action, metadata,
   independent record, corroboration, or contradiction.
6. **Resolve the date basis.** Keep event, recording, publication, archive, and review
   dates separate.
7. **Check sensitivity and conflicts.** Minimize private information and preserve
   materially different accounts.
8. **Quarantine unsafe machine-only material.** Do not publish machine-only serious
   wrongdoing, sexualized-minor claims, or invasive private or medical detail. A benign
   public lead must name its raw-media status and review queue.
9. **Publish with a precise citation.** Record the source ID, locator, check status,
   reviewer, and review date.
</Steps>

## Dates, contradictions, and transcript corrections

A date taken only from an archive filename is labeled **filename date**. If the exact
event or publication date cannot be established, the wiki uses a range, `circa`, or
`unknown` rather than false precision.

Video titles are not treated as conclusions. If a later source changes an earlier
account, both are dated and attributed. The wiki does not silently choose the more
dramatic version.

Quoted words are checked against the media. Unclear audio is marked `[unclear]`,
conservatively paraphrased, or omitted. Machine-transcript corrections do not alter
the preserved raw snapshot.

## Privacy and sensitive material

The wiki does not republish home addresses, private contact details, financial account
data, leaked intimate material, or non-public legal names merely because another site
contains them. An allegation may be recorded with an adjacent warning when its exact
source is attributed, its truth is labeled unverified, disputed, or corroborated, its
corroboration status is stated, and a relevant response is included or the page says
none was located. A corroborated allegation must still be labeled as an allegation and
identify the independent support. A warning does not turn the allegation into fact or
excuse doxxing, intimate material, diagnoses, threats, or harassment-oriented detail.
Machine output alone is never enough to publish a serious allegation, a sexualized
claim involving a minor, or invasive private, relationship, financial, travel, or
medical detail.

The complete contributor rules are maintained in the repository's editorial policy.
