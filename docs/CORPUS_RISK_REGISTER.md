# Corpus engineering risk register

This register tracks conditions that could make the corpus inaccurate, unsafe to
publish, irreproducible, or too large to build. A green test suite does not close a
risk unless the named failure mode has its own test or measured gate.

Statuses are `open`, `in progress`, `mitigated`, or `accepted`. “Mitigated” means a
control and regression test exist; it does not promise that every future source or
model output is correct.

## Operating rule

A milestone can advance with open lower-priority risks when the next work is private
and reversible. Publication cannot advance past an open critical risk, and corpus-wide
backfill cannot start while `WEB-01`, `SCH-01`, or the relevant model-quality gate is
open.
