# Rights and reuse

This repository is publicly readable, but no repository-wide software, content, or
media license has been selected. Public access to the source does not by itself grant
permission to copy, redistribute, or create derivative works from it.

## Repository material

- Project-authored code and text have no declared reuse license at this time.
- Third-party names, marks, links, quotations, screenshots, video frames, artwork,
  and other media remain subject to their respective owners' rights.
- A provenance record documents where an asset came from and why it is present; it
  is not a claim that the project owns the asset or can license it to others.
- No license that may later be added to project-authored material should be assumed
  to cover separately identified third-party media.

When reusing material outside this repository, check the individual file's notices,
its provenance record, and the original source. If no permission is stated, do not
assume one.

## Contributions

Only submit code, writing, or media that you created or are authorized to
contribute. Prefer linking to a public source over copying it into the repository.
For a proposed image or other media asset, document the source, rights or permission
basis, transformations, and cryptographic hash as required by
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

Maintainers may decline or remove material whose ownership, permission, privacy, or
provenance is unclear. Do not submit leaked, private, intimate, or access-controlled
material even if it is available elsewhere online.

For a rights, attribution, privacy, or removal concern that can be explained entirely
with public information, use the repository's content-correction issue form. If the
report would expose private information or unpublished evidence, follow
[`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) and do not post the details in a public issue.
